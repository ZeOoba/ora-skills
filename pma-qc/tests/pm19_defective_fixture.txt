MANAGEMENT AGREEMENT
The Owner hereby appoints the Agent exclusively to rent, lease, operate and manage the above
referenced Property. This agreement shall automatically renew for annual periods.
Agent is authorized to make or cause to be made all ordinary repairs and alterations to the
Property, to hire such contractors and vendors as Agent deems necessary, and to purchase
supplies and materials therefor. Agent shall obtain the prior written approval of Owner for
any single expenditure in excess of $2,000.00, except in the case of an emergency, in which
event Agent may act in its sole discretion.
Agent shall receive four (4%) percent of gross monies collected or $850 monthly, whichever is
greater. Owner shall save Agent harmless from all damage suits.
Owner Signature: ______________________
Agent shall render monthly statements of receipts and disbursements to Owner and shall
remit the net proceeds thereof. Agent shall deposit all receipts in a trust account at a
bank of Agent's selection. Agent shall have the authority to sign, renew and cancel leases
for the Property and to institute and prosecute actions to evict tenants and recover
possession of the premises. Owner shall assume all contracts entered into by Agent upon
termination of this agreement. This agreement may be terminated by either party upon sixty
days written notice. Agent shall be compensated at the rate of forty five dollars per hour
for construction supervision services rendered in connection with capital projects at the
Property. Owner shall carry public liability insurance naming Agent as an additional
insured party thereunder at all times during the term hereof and any renewal term.
