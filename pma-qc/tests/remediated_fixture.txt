PROPERTY MANAGEMENT AGREEMENT

FAIRGROUND VILLAGE CENTER LLC - hereinafter called "Owner"
Meridian Property Services, LLC - hereinafter called "Agent"

1. APPOINTMENT. Owner appoints Agent to manage the properties listed on Exhibit A.
Agent's appointment as leasing agent is a separate grant, made property by property
on Exhibit A, and may be modified by Owner as to any property on thirty (30) days'
written notice without affecting Agent's management appointment as to that or any
other property. Owner may remove any property from Exhibit A upon thirty (30) days'
written notice, whereupon this agreement shall terminate as to that property only.

2. TERM. The initial term runs through December 31, 2027. This agreement may be
extended for successive annual periods by written agreement of the parties. Not
later than ninety (90) days before each anniversary, Agent shall deliver to Owner a
written summary of fees charged during the preceding twelve months by category.
Either party may terminate on sixty (60) days' written notice. Owner may terminate
immediately for cause upon fraud, misapplication of funds, loss of license, or
failure to maintain the required bond or insurance.

3. AUTHORITY. Agent shall not have the authority to execute any lease, lease
amendment, renewal, extension, purchase and sale agreement, or other instrument on
behalf of Owner. All documentation shall be executed solely by Owner or its duly
authorized representative. Agent shall not commence, settle, compromise, or release
any action in Owner's name without Owner's prior written approval. Owner shall
select counsel.

4. EXPENDITURES. Agent shall obtain Owner's prior written approval for any
expenditure exceeding $5,000 for any one item or $25,000 in the aggregate in any
calendar year, excepting only emergency expenditures reasonably necessary to
prevent imminent injury to persons or material damage to the Property, of which
Agent shall notify Owner within twenty-four (24) hours.

5. FUNDS. All funds collected for Owner's account shall be held in a segregated
trust account in Owner's name at a federally insured institution, shall not be
commingled with Agent's funds or with funds of any other owner, and any interest
earned thereon shall accrue to Owner or be credited against fees next due. Agent
shall deliver written notice of any deficit with the monthly statement. Owner shall
fund within fifteen (15) days after receipt of such notice. Amounts not funded
within ten (10) days after a second written notice shall bear interest at the
lesser of one percent (1%) per month, simple interest and non-compounding, or the
maximum rate permitted by law. Agent shall remit net proceeds to Owner by the
fifteenth (15th) day of each month.

6. RECORDS. Agent shall maintain complete books and records for the Property,
retained for not less than four (4) years. Owner may inspect and audit such records
upon five (5) business days' notice. If an audit discloses an overcharge exceeding
three percent (3%), Agent shall reimburse the cost of the audit. Agent shall
deliver a proposed annual operating budget not later than November 1 for Owner's
approval, monthly variance report against the approved budget, and a year-end
package including all Forms 1099. All information regarding the Property, its
tenants, and its financial performance shall be treated as confidential.

7. INSURANCE AND BOND. Agent shall maintain a fidelity bond in the amount of
$500,000 covering employees handling Owner's funds, commercial general liability
insurance of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence, and professional liability
(errors and omissions) insurance of not less than $1,000,000, and shall deliver
certificates evidencing the same upon execution and annually thereafter.

8. INDEMNITY. Owner shall indemnify Agent, provided that Owner's indemnity shall
not extend to any claim arising from Agent's negligence, willful misconduct, breach
of this agreement, or violation of law. Agent shall indemnify and hold Owner
harmless from any claim arising from Agent's negligent or wrongful acts or
omissions, or those of its employees and subcontractors.

9. FEES. "Gross Monies Collected" means base rent, percentage rent, and recurring
operating-expense, tax, and insurance reimbursements actually collected from
tenants at the Property during the month, and excludes security deposits, insurance
and condemnation proceeds, proceeds of any sale or financing, capital
contributions, tenant improvement allowances, tax-appeal refunds, and late fees.
For management: four percent (4%) of Gross Monies Collected.

"Gross Lease Value" means aggregate base rent payable over the initial stated term,
excluding option, renewal, and expansion periods, operating expense reimbursements,
percentage rent, and any period of abated rent, and net of any tenant improvement
allowance funded by Owner. For leasing: four percent (4%) of Gross Lease Value,
payable half upon full execution and half upon rent commencement. If the tenant
defaults and the lease is terminated within twelve (12) months after rent
commencement, Agent shall refund a pro rata portion of the commission. Any
cooperating or outside broker engaged by Agent shall be compensated from Agent's
fee, and shall not increase the aggregate leasing commission payable by Owner. No
commission shall be payable with respect to any lease executed after the
termination of this agreement. Renewals, extensions, and expansions shall be
handled by Owner or by such party as Owner may designate, at no cost to Owner.

Agent's construction supervision fee shall not exceed four percent (4%) of hard
costs actually incurred, shall apply only to capital projects exceeding $25,000
approved in advance by Owner. Agent shall disclose in writing any interest it or
any affiliate holds in a contractor, vendor, or supplier engaged at the Property,
and shall pass through all third-party costs at actual cost without markup.

The casualty fee shall be ten (10) percent of gross billings collected from the
insurer, shall be the sole compensation payable to Agent in connection with a
casualty restoration, and shall be credited against any construction supervision
fee otherwise payable for the same work.

10. ASSIGNMENT. Agent may not assign this agreement or delegate its duties without
Owner's prior written consent. A transfer of a majority of the ownership interests
in Agent, or a merger or sale of substantially all of Agent's assets, shall
constitute an assignment.

11. TERMINATION. Agent shall deliver to Owner, within fifteen (15) days after
termination, all leases, tenant files, security deposits, a final accounting, keys,
plans, warranties, and a complete schedule of all service contracts entered into on
Owner's behalf. Owner shall assume only those service contracts identified on a
schedule delivered to and approved by Owner in advance, and in no event any
contract not terminable on sixty (60) days' notice.

12. NOTICES. Notices shall be delivered to the parties addressed as follows, by
hand, nationally recognized overnight courier, or certified mail.

13. MISCELLANEOUS. This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of
Ohio. Venue shall lie exclusively in Franklin County, Ohio. The prevailing party
shall recover its reasonable attorneys' fees. The parties waive any right to trial
by jury.
