MANAGEMENT AGREEMENT
Agent is authorized to contract for ordinary repairs and improvements to the Property.
Every such contract shall name Owner as the contracting party, and Agent shall execute it
for and on behalf of Owner as a disclosed agent. Owner shall be the contracting party on
all vendor and trade agreements.
Agent shall obtain the prior written approval of Owner for any single expenditure in excess
of $10,000, which threshold shall be adjusted annually by CPI, and Owner shall countersign
the trade contract for any such expenditure. In an emergency Agent may act to prevent
imminent harm and shall promptly notify Owner within twenty-four hours.
Agent shall render monthly statements of receipts and disbursements to Owner and shall
remit the net proceeds thereof to Owner within ten days of month end. Agent shall deposit
all receipts in a segregated trust account at a bank approved by Owner in writing, and all
interest earned thereon shall be the property of Owner. Agent shall have no authority to
sign, renew or cancel any lease, which authority Owner expressly reserves to itself. Owner
shall assume only those contracts identified on a schedule delivered to Owner and approved
in writing. This agreement may be terminated by either party upon sixty days written notice
delivered to the notice address set forth below. Agent shall be compensated for construction
supervision at rates set out on Exhibit C, adjusted annually. Agent shall maintain errors and
omissions coverage and a fidelity bond throughout the term hereof and any renewal term.
