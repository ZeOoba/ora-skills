# Clause modules — ORA landlord lease form

Parameterized, landlord-drafted modules for terms the ORA master does not yet carry as tokenized blocks.
Each was drafted, validated (docx validate.py + lease-form-qc 1/52) and rendered on the 2207 Northwood
Suite 15A lease (2026-09-07). Placement is by **heading title**, never by section number, so the module
survives renumbering. `{PARAM}` = fill from the deal-terms register. `**…**` = bold defined term (engine markup).

Every module pairs the tenant's ask with the landlord conditions that bound it. Do not strip the conditions
to "simplify" — they are the point. Screen any tenant-side edit against the six ratified positions first.

---

## M-01 Definitions block  (§1, new Heading-2 "Definitions")
Cures scanner A5a/A5b/A5c/F12. `"Term" means` phrasing is required — the scanner tests for it.

> **Definitions.**  As used in this Lease: “**Building**” means the building situated at {ADDRESS}, in which the Demised Premises are located.  “**Project**” means the project consisting of {BUILDING_COUNT} buildings located at {PROJECT_ADDRESS}, containing approximately {PROJECT_RSF} rentable square feet in the aggregate, together with the land, parking areas and common areas appurtenant thereto, known as {PROJECT_NAME}.  “**Business Day**” means Monday through Friday, excluding days on which banks in the State of {STATE} are authorized or required to close.  “**Lease Term**” means the Primary Term, the Option Term if exercised, and any holdover period.  “**Governmental Agency**” means any federal, state, county, municipal or other governmental or quasi-governmental authority having jurisdiction over the Project.  “**Building Standard**” means the quality and quantity of materials and finishes designated by Landlord from time to time as standard for the Building.  “**Premises**” and “**Demised Premises**” are used interchangeably.

Also in §1: stipulate SF — *"The rentable area of the Demised Premises and of the Project as stated in this Lease is stipulated and agreed by the parties for all purposes of this Lease and shall not be subject to remeasurement."* — and carve Landlord Work out of the as-is sentence: *"Except for the Landlord Work described in the section of this Lease entitled “LANDLORD WORK,” Tenant accepts possession … “as is” …"*

## M-02 Term by formula + Delay in Delivery  (§2(a), §2(b) — replace in place)
Use whenever Landlord Work precedes delivery. Cures A10 (circular delay clause); preserves the full term for the tenant; Tenant Delay and force majeure protect the landlord; termination is the tenant's sole remedy.

> **Term.**  … the term of this Lease shall commence on the Commencement Date and shall expire at 11:59 p.m. on the last day of the {TERM_MONTHS_WORDS} ({TERM_MONTHS}th) full calendar month following the Commencement Date (the “**Expiration Date**”), unless sooner terminated or extended in accordance with the terms hereof (such term, exclusive of any Option Term, the “**Primary Term**”).  The “**Commencement Date**” shall be the later of (i) {TARGET_DATE} (the “**Target Commencement Date**”) and (ii) the Delivery Date (as defined in the section of this Lease entitled “LANDLORD WORK”).  The parties anticipate that the Commencement Date will be {TARGET_DATE} and the Expiration Date will be {ANTICIPATED_EXPIRATION}.  If the Commencement Date is not {TARGET_DATE}, the parties shall, upon Landlord’s request, execute a memorandum confirming the Commencement Date and the Expiration Date, but Tenant’s failure to do so shall not affect either date.

> **Delay in Delivery.**  If for any reason Landlord is unable to deliver possession of the Demised Premises to Tenant with the Landlord Work Substantially Complete on or before the Target Commencement Date, Landlord shall not be liable for any damage thereby, this Lease shall not be void or voidable, and Tenant shall not be obligated to pay Monthly Rent or other monetary sums until the Commencement Date; provided, however, that if the delay results from any act or omission of Tenant or its agents, employees or contractors, including any change to the Landlord Work requested by Tenant or any delay in Tenant furnishing information or approvals reasonably requested by Landlord (“**Tenant Delay**”), the Commencement Date shall be the date on which the Delivery Date would have occurred but for the Tenant Delay.  If Tenant, with Landlord’s consent, takes possession prior to the Commencement Date, Tenant shall do so subject to all of the terms and conditions hereof and shall pay Monthly Rent for the period prior to the Commencement Date at the same rate, prorated on a daily basis.  If the Delivery Date has not occurred within {OUTSIDE_DAYS} days after the Target Commencement Date, as such period may be extended by Tenant Delay and by the force majeure provision of this Lease, then Tenant, as its sole and exclusive remedy, may terminate this Lease by written notice delivered to Landlord within five (5) Business Days after such {OUTSIDE_DAYS}th day and before the Delivery Date, whereupon Landlord shall return any monies previously deposited by Tenant and the parties shall have no further rights or liabilities hereunder.

If there is **no** Landlord Work, keep the form's fixed dates and drop this module (but still rename "Delay in Commencement" → "Delay in Delivery" and measure the outside date from the Target Commencement Date).

## M-03 Option to Extend  (§2, new Heading-2 after "Lease Year")
Departure from ratified position 6 — use only when the LOI grants it; log in the register. Rent lives in the §3(c) schedule (M-04), never here.

> **Option to Extend.**  Provided that (i) no Event of Default exists, and no event has occurred which with notice or the passage of time would constitute an Event of Default, either on the date Tenant delivers the Option Notice or on the last day of the Primary Term, (ii) Tenant has not been late in the payment of Monthly Rent more than twice during the twelve (12) months preceding the Option Notice, (iii) the Tenant originally named in this Lease (and not any assignee or subtenant) is in occupancy of the entire Demised Premises, and (iv) this Lease has not been assigned nor the Demised Premises sublet, Tenant shall have {OPTION_COUNT_WORDS} ({OPTION_COUNT}) option to extend the term of this Lease for {OPTION_COUNT_WORDS} ({OPTION_COUNT}) additional period of {OPTION_MONTHS_WORDS} ({OPTION_MONTHS}) months (the “**Option Term**”) commencing on the day after the Expiration Date.  Tenant shall exercise the option, if at all, by written notice to Landlord (the “**Option Notice**”) delivered not earlier than {WINDOW_OPEN} months and not later than {WINDOW_CLOSE} months before the Expiration Date, time being of the essence; if the Option Notice is not delivered within that window the option shall lapse and be of no further force or effect.  The Option Term shall be upon all of the terms and conditions of this Lease, except that (A) Monthly Rent during the Option Term shall be as set forth in the rent schedule in the section of this Lease entitled “RENT,” (B) Landlord shall have no obligation to perform any work, provide any allowance or grant any other concession, and (C) there shall be no further option to extend.  If an Event of Default occurs after the Option Notice is delivered and before the Option Term commences, Landlord may, at its election, by written notice to Tenant, cancel the exercise of the option, in which case this Lease shall expire on the Expiration Date.  The option is personal to the Tenant originally named in this Lease.  References in this Lease to the “term” or the “term of this Lease” include the Option Term if the option is exercised.

State check: MD — recording required only for terms > 7 years (Real Prop. § 3-101); OH — total possible term > 3 years triggers R.C. 5301.01 acknowledgment.

## M-05 Gross lease §4  (retitle "TAXES, INSURANCE AND OPERATING COSTS"; (a) gross, (b) definitions kept, (c) exclusions)
Departure from ratified position 1 — only when the LOI says taxes/insurance/CAM are inside the rent. Keep the definitions: Proportionate Share is the water/sewer key; "Taxes" feeds the personal-property carve-out. The phrase "no annual estimate or reconciliation" is what clears scanner A6 truthfully.

> **Gross Rent.**  Monthly Rent includes Tenant’s Proportionate Share of Taxes, of the premiums for the insurance Landlord is required to carry under this Lease, and of Operating Costs.  Except as provided in subsection (c) of this Section and elsewhere in this Lease with respect to costs caused by or separately attributable to Tenant, Tenant shall not be required to pay any additional rent on account of Taxes, insurance premiums or Operating Costs, and there shall be no base-year or expense-stop pass-through, and no annual estimate or reconciliation of Operating Costs, under this Lease.

> (b) — retain "Operating Costs", "Taxes", "Tenant's Proportionate Share" ({PCT}%, being {RSF} / {PROJECT_RSF} rentable square feet — *stipulated*, not "estimated"). Replace the gross-up sentence with: *Operating Costs shall not include any cost that is separately billed to, or paid directly by, Tenant or any other tenant of the Project.*

> **Exclusions.**  Notwithstanding subsection (a) of this Section, Tenant shall pay, as additional rent within fifteen (15) days after invoice: (i) all taxes and assessments levied on Tenant’s personal property, trade fixtures, inventory and Alterations, and any sales, use, rent or excise tax imposed on Tenant or on the rent payable by Tenant (other than Landlord’s income taxes); (ii) any increase in the premium for insurance carried by Landlord to the extent caused by Tenant’s particular use or occupancy of the Demised Premises or by Tenant’s failure to comply with this Lease; (iii) all utilities and services furnished to or consumed in the Demised Premises as provided in the section of this Lease entitled “SERVICES AND UTILITIES”; (iv) the cost of any repair, maintenance or service that this Lease allocates to Tenant or that is necessitated by the act or omission of Tenant or its agents, employees, contractors, invitees or vehicles; and (v) any charge under the section of this Lease entitled “PARKING.”  Nothing in this Section limits Landlord’s right to recover such amounts, and such amounts are not Operating Costs for purposes of subsection (a) of this Section.

For **modified gross** (position 1 default) use the master's base-year block instead; keep (c) as the exclusions.

## M-06 Utilities  (§10(a) replace, §10(b) insert; existing cessation clause becomes (c))
> **Electricity; Other Utilities.**  The Demised Premises are separately metered for electricity.  Tenant shall, effective as of the Commencement Date, place the electric account for the Demised Premises in Tenant’s name with the applicable utility provider and shall pay directly to the provider, before delinquency, all charges for electricity consumed in the Demised Premises.  Tenant shall likewise contract and pay directly for telephone, internet, gas (if any) and any other utility or service serving the Demised Premises other than water and sewer.  Landlord shall not be required to furnish any utility or service to the Demised Premises except as expressly provided in subsection (b) of this Section.  If Tenant fails to place the electric account in its name, Landlord may pay the charges and Tenant shall reimburse Landlord as additional rent, together with an administrative fee of ten percent (10%), within fifteen (15) days after invoice.

> **Water and Sewer.**  Landlord shall furnish water and sewer service to the Demised Premises for drinking, lavatory, toilet and ordinary cleaning purposes through the existing fixtures.  Tenant shall pay to Landlord, as additional rent within fifteen (15) days after invoice, Tenant’s pro rata share of the water and sewer charges (including any fixed, capacity or benefit charges) billed to Landlord for the {Building|meter serving the Demised Premises}, such share being the ratio of the rentable square footage of the Demised Premises to the rentable square footage of all premises served by the same meter; provided that if Landlord reasonably determines that Tenant’s use of water is disproportionate to its square footage, Landlord may (i) equitably adjust Tenant’s share to reflect such use, or (ii) install a submeter for the Demised Premises at Tenant’s expense, in which case Tenant shall pay the metered charges.  Landlord may invoice such charges monthly, quarterly or on the utility’s billing cycle.  Water and sewer charges are not Operating Costs.

Flat-fee variant (master default, clears B8): *"…Tenant shall pay a flat rate of ${FEE} per month for water and sewer…"*

## M-07 Maintenance — LOI "landlord takes HVAC and major mechanicals" split  (§11(a), (b) replace; new (c))
Use only when the LOI puts HVAC/electrical/plumbing on the landlord. The ORA form default keeps HVAC on the tenant (with F17 PM contract). The "major" line is systems vs. fixtures. Include the explicit HVAC-replacement sentence (B5).

> **Landlord’s Obligations.**  Landlord shall maintain, repair and, when necessary in Landlord’s reasonable judgment, replace, in good and usable condition consistent with comparable flex buildings in the {MARKET} market: (i) the roof, roof membrane, roof structure and supports, foundation, floor slab, exterior walls and all structural elements of the Building; (ii) the exterior doors (including any overhead door) and exterior windows of the Demised Premises, including frames and glazing, except glass broken by Tenant or its agents, employees, contractors or invitees; (iii) the heating, ventilating and air-conditioning system(s) serving the Demised Premises, including the {heat pump} system(s) and ducting (the “**HVAC System**”); (iv) the electrical system of the Building to and including the panel serving the Demised Premises, and the water supply, sanitary sewer and other plumbing lines within the walls, under the slab and outside the Demised Premises to the point of connection with fixtures in the Demised Premises (collectively, the “**Major Mechanical Systems**”); (v) the parking areas, driveways, curbs, sidewalks, exterior lighting, lawn and landscaping of the Project, including snow and ice removal from the parking areas and sidewalks; and (vi) utility lines serving the Demised Premises to the extent not maintained by a public utility.  Landlord shall be responsible for replacement of the heating, ventilating and air-conditioning equipment serving the Demised Premises at the end of its useful life, unless replacement is necessitated by a cause described in subsection (c) of this Section.  Landlord’s obligations under this subsection shall arise only after Tenant gives Landlord written notice of the need for the repair, and Landlord shall have a reasonable time thereafter to perform.  Landlord shall not be liable for, and Tenant shall not be entitled to any abatement of rent on account of, any interruption or inconvenience resulting from any such repair or from Landlord’s failure to perform, subject to the section of this Lease entitled “SERVICES AND UTILITIES.”  Landlord’s obligations under this subsection are at Landlord’s cost and are not chargeable to Tenant, subject to subsection (c) of this Section.

> **Tenant’s Obligations.**  Tenant shall, at its sole cost, keep the interior of the Demised Premises and the fixtures and equipment therein in clean, safe and sanitary condition, shall suffer no waste or injury thereto, and shall make and pay for all maintenance and repairs to the interior of the Demised Premises, including: interior walls, floors and floor coverings, ceilings and ceiling tiles (except tiles damaged by a roof leak), interior doors, frames and hardware; plumbing fixtures within the Demised Premises (faucets, toilets, drains and traps, including clearing stoppages caused by Tenant); electrical outlets, switches, light fixtures, ballasts and the replacement of lamps and bulbs; interior locks and keys; fire extinguishers; janitorial service and trash removal; pest control within the Demised Premises; {the replacement of HVAC System air filters not less often than every three (3) months;} and all special systems, equipment and improvements installed by or for Tenant.  Tenant shall promptly notify Landlord in writing of any condition requiring repair by Landlord under subsection (a) of this Section.

> **Tenant-Caused Repairs.**  Notwithstanding subsection (a) of this Section, any maintenance, repair or replacement otherwise required of Landlord that is necessitated by the negligence, misuse, abuse or intentional act of Tenant or its agents, employees, contractors, invitees or vehicles, or by Tenant’s failure to perform its obligations under subsection (b) of this Section, shall be performed by Landlord at Tenant’s sole cost, payable as additional rent within fifteen (15) days after invoice.
