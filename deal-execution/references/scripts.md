## audit_completeness.py — what each check catches

1. **Exhibit population** — an exhibit can be present, correctly titled, referenced throughout, and completely EMPTY. An exhibit whose only content is an empty table grid (zero rows, every cell blank, struck-only cells, or only a DMS stamp or an empty content-control prompt) is EMPTY and blocks (Gabriel's in-turn ruling, 2026-11-06): a table counts toward population only through its visible text or images. Trace what depends on each empty exhibit before dismissing it; definitions, representation carve-outs, and delivery obligations routinely hang off exhibits and fail silently when blank. A `THIN` exhibit (a bare image, a parcel number, or a table with a header row and no data rows, such as a skeleton rent roll) is not automatically a defect, but ask whether a third party could draft from it — a recordable instrument cannot be prepared from an aerial markup, and a deed cannot be prepared from a parcel number.
2. **Cross-references** — exhibits cited but absent, exhibits present but never cited, and section references that do not resolve.
3. **Unfilled blanks** — placeholder runs and open definitions. Body blanks are defects; signature-block blanks are expected but must be completed consistently at execution. Inside a post-signature exhibit (one whose heading comes after IN WITNESS WHEREOF; an exhibit list or exhibit before it never swallows the signature page) every blank is a body blank and blocks (Gabriel's in-turn ruling, 2026-11-06) unless its line is a genuine signature/notary/recording-form line. `_is_signature_line` splits paragraphs at soft breaks and checks each line: a line with `$` is never exempt; every `Label:` on the line must be a signature/notary/recording label (`By`, `Name`, `Title`, `Its`, `Authorized Signatory`, `Attest`, `Witness(es)`, `Print`, `Date Signed`, `Name and Title`, party labels `Grantor`/`Grantee`/`Seller`/`Purchaser`/`Buyer`/`Landlord`/`Tenant`/`Assignor`/`Assignee`, the notary labels such as `My commission expires`/`Commission No.`/`My County of Residence`, and `This instrument prepared by`/`Send tax statements to`/`Return to`); any parenthetical other than `(SEAL)` disqualifies it; venue lines must have the venue shape (`STATE OF OHIO ) ss: COUNTY OF ____ )`), so `State of Ohio, County of ..., Parcel No. ____` blocks; notary wording counts only in its anchored title or sentence forms (`Notary Public ____`, `Acknowledged before me ...`, `Sworn to before me ...`, `On ____, before me, ..., personally appeared ...`); entity lines (`____, a ____ limited liability company`) and executed/dated/receipt day-of lines are exempt; a bare `_____` is exempt as a paragraph but not inside a table cell. A heading table's own rows are scanned too. **Form-of exemption** (Gabriel's second in-turn ruling, 2026-11-06): an exhibit whose caption (one of the first two blocks after the heading, or a later paragraph of a heading table) starts with `Form of` (optionally after `Exhibit X –` or `(`) and holds no blank is a template completed at closing; its blanks are reported as `form_blanks` and never block. Blanks on the signature page itself, and in exhibits placed before it, behave as before.
