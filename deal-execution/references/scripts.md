## audit_completeness.py — what each check catches

1. **Exhibit population** — an exhibit can be present, correctly titled, referenced throughout, and completely EMPTY. An exhibit whose only content is an empty table grid (zero rows, every cell blank, struck-only cells, or only a DMS stamp or an empty content-control prompt) is EMPTY and blocks (Gabriel's in-turn ruling, 2026-11-06): a table counts toward population only through its visible text or images, and underscore runs never count (a rule-only exhibit is EMPTY; a parcel number plus a rule is THIN; v1.15). Trace what depends on each empty exhibit before dismissing it; definitions, representation carve-outs, and delivery obligations routinely hang off exhibits and fail silently when blank. A `THIN` exhibit (a bare image, a parcel number, or a table with a header row and no data rows, such as a skeleton rent roll) is not automatically a defect, but ask whether a third party could draft from it — a recordable instrument cannot be prepared from an aerial markup, and a deed cannot be prepared from a parcel number.
2. **Cross-references** — exhibits cited but absent, exhibits present but never cited, and section references that do not resolve.
3. **Unfilled blanks** — placeholder runs and open definitions. Body blanks are defects; signature-block blanks are expected but must be completed consistently at execution. Inside a post-signature exhibit (one whose heading comes after IN WITNESS WHEREOF; an exhibit list or exhibit before it never swallows the signature page) every blank is a body blank and blocks (Gabriel's in-turn ruling, 2026-11-06) unless its line is a genuine signature/notary/recording-form line. `_classify_line` splits paragraphs at soft breaks and classifies each line. *Exempt on its own:* a signature label with nothing but the blank (`By`, `Name`, `Title`, `Its`, `Date`, `Authorized Signatory`, `Attest`, `Witness(es)`, `Print`, `Date Signed`, `Name and Title`, the notary labels such as `My commission expires`/`Commission No.`/`County of Residence`, `This instrument prepared by`); a bare rule (as a paragraph or a one-cell table; in a data-captioned exhibit, and after a Form-of region ends, only inside a signature stack: a strong cue judged on the whole line — an unfilled `By: ____`; a filled `By:` naming a person (`By: Jane Q. Doe, Manager`) or with a `Name:`/`Title:`/`Its:` line directly beside it; a seal-only line (`____ (SEAL)`, `(L.S.)`); a Notary Public title or commission line; a full venue line (`STATE OF OHIO )`, `COUNTY OF FRANKLIN ) ss:`); or `(Signature)`/Signature/Authorized Signatory/Printed Name as a whole label followed by nothing, a blank or a personal name — never a mid-prose match (`…, to wit:`, `County of Franklin (the "County")`, `Monument stamped (SEAL)`, `I hereby certify …`, `Signature Aviation LLC`, `By: Acme LLC`), carrying no `$` or value word — reached through at most three adjacent signature-label lines, with at most one rule per stack; filled or unfilled `Name:`/`Title:`/`Date:`/`Dated:`/`Witness`/`Attest` lines never clear it, and an unfilled `Name:`/`Title:`/`Date:` there needs the same stack; else a body blank, v1.15); `(SEAL)`/`(Notary Seal)` lines; venue lines with at most two clauses each ending in `)` or `ss` (`STATE OF OHIO ) ss: COUNTY OF ____ )`, `____ COUNTY )`) and the MD `STATE OF …, COUNTY OF …, TO WIT:`; notary title lines (`Notary Public ____`, `____, Notary Public`); executed/dated/receipt day-of lines. *Exempt next to a notary cue* (within three lines; a cue is a Notary Public title, a commission/term/appointment/county-of-residence line, a venue line, an MD TO WIT line or a notary seal — never another sentence): the acknowledgment sentences used in OH (incl. statutory `(name of officer)` parentheticals), IN (incl. the SSN-redaction affirmation), MD (`I HEREBY CERTIFY`), MO (`On this … before me appeared`), KS (`This instrument was acknowledged`), WI (`Personally came before me`) and TN, plus `Witness my hand`, `In testimony whereof`, `Given under my hand`; these may run to 900 characters (TN TCA 66-22-107, MD/MO corporate forms), every other line is capped at 400; a bare `STATE/COUNTY OF ____` likewise needs a notary cue. *Exempt next to a signature cue* (a label followed by a blank, `By: ____`, `Name: ____`, or a seal-only line — never prose like `Its lease expires`): party labels (`Grantor`, `Grantee`, `Seller`, `Purchaser`, `Buyer`, `Landlord`, `Tenant`, `Assignor`, `Assignee`, `Return to`, tax-statement lines) on an adjacent line, with nothing after the blank; entity lines (`____, a ____ limited liability company`) and a bare rule in a multi-cell table within three lines (same cell for cell lines). None of these context exemptions applies in a data-captioned exhibit. *Body:* everything else — any line with `$`, a value word after its signature label (rent, rental, rentable, price, parcel, lot, suite, unit, term, deposit, square feet/footage, commencement, expiration, closing, effective, consideration, amount, balance, fee, premises, address, PIN, tax id …), a second non-signature label, or a non-seal parenthetical. Every pattern is anchored and bounded and cue lookup is linear. A heading table's own rows are scanned too. **Form-of exemption with data-caption override** (Gabriel's in-turn rulings, 2026-11-06: Form-of exemption; data captions override it): an exhibit whose caption — the first non-blank block after the heading (spacers, page breaks and a `to Purchase Agreement` heading continuation skipped; soft breaks joined) — reads `Form of X`, `(Form of X)`, `[FORM OF] X`, `X (Form)`, `Exhibit B – Form of X` or `Exhibit B to …: Form of X`, holds no blank, does not say `attached` and has no colon after `Form of`, is a template completed at closing; its blanks are reported as `form_blanks` and never block, until the next block that opens with EXHIBIT/SCHEDULE/ANNEX/APPENDIX/ATTACHMENT/RIDER (whatever follows) or is a short block carrying a data caption — what follows is judged as data; an inline cross-reference to a separately attached exhibit or schedule — `Legal Description: See Exhibit A attached hereto.` (label, colon or dash, then see / described in / set forth on Exhibit|Schedule <id> attached, or attached as Exhibit|Schedule <id>) — does not end the region; it must also name an Exhibit with exactly one EXHIBIT heading, outside the form exhibit; `see Schedule 1 below/following/herein`, `attached as Schedule 1 below`, any Schedule reference (an unrelated `SCHEDULE 1` may sit inside any exhibit), or a reference to a missing or duplicated exhibit heading ends the region (v1.15). A filled `By:` or signature caption clears a rule only with a personal name (Unicode and all-caps accepted; entity words and placeholders such as `To Be Provided`, `See Schedule`, `TBD`, `To Follow`, `Not Yet Available`, `Forthcoming`, `Counsel` rejected); seal-only lines include `[SEAL]` and `(Corporate Seal)`; `Dated`/`Executed`/`Signed` link a stack only in label / `as of` / `this … day of` shape. Accepted residuals: capitalised entity/geographic names on a filled `By:`/Printed Name line read as a person (`By: North Coast`, `By: Acme Warehouse`; origin/main too); a venue line alone clears an adjacent rule (origin/main too); after a valid single-target Exhibit cross-reference, headingless inline data later in the Form-of exhibit is judged as form (follows from the C2 decision); an all-caps signer line with no `By:` label blocks (fail closed); the flaky `rulings4` 10k-venue-line timing test (about 0.86s median on origin/main vs a 1.0s threshold) is untouched. Form-field blanks inside a valid Form-of region (`Rent: $____`) are form blanks by design. A data caption — rent roll, legal description (`legal descr`), property/premises/land description, any `schedule`, tenant list/roster/schedule, list of leases; and leases, service contracts, permitted exceptions, lease abstract or a personal property list unless a form noun such as assignment is present — is never a form. Blanks on the signature page itself, and in exhibits placed before it, behave as before.
