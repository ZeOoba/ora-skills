## audit_completeness.py — what each check catches

1. **Exhibit population** — an exhibit can be present, correctly titled, referenced throughout, and completely EMPTY. An exhibit whose only content is an empty table grid (zero rows, every cell blank, struck-only cells, or only a DMS stamp or an empty content-control prompt) is EMPTY and blocks (Gabriel's in-turn ruling, 2026-11-06): a table counts toward population only through its visible text or images. Trace what depends on each empty exhibit before dismissing it; definitions, representation carve-outs, and delivery obligations routinely hang off exhibits and fail silently when blank. A `THIN` exhibit (a bare image, a parcel number, or a table with a header row and no data rows, such as a skeleton rent roll) is not automatically a defect, but ask whether a third party could draft from it — a recordable instrument cannot be prepared from an aerial markup, and a deed cannot be prepared from a parcel number.
2. **Cross-references** — exhibits cited but absent, exhibits present but never cited, and section references that do not resolve.
3. **Unfilled blanks** — placeholder runs and open definitions. Body blanks are defects; signature-block blanks are expected but must be completed consistently at execution. Inside a post-signature exhibit (one whose heading comes after IN WITNESS WHEREOF; an exhibit list or exhibit before it never swallows the signature page) every blank is a body blank and blocks (Gabriel's in-turn ruling, 2026-11-06) unless its line is a genuine signature/notary/recording-form line. `_classify_line` splits paragraphs at soft breaks and classifies each line. *Exempt on its own:* a signature label with nothing but the blank (`By`, `Name`, `Title`, `Its`, `Date`, `Authorized Signatory`, `Attest`, `Witness(es)`, `Print`, `Date Signed`, `Name and Title`, the notary labels such as `My commission expires`/`Commission No.`/`County of Residence`, `This instrument prepared by`); a bare rule (as a paragraph or a one-cell table); `(SEAL)`/`(Notary Seal)` lines; venue lines with at most two clauses each ending in `)` or `ss` (`STATE OF OHIO ) ss: COUNTY OF ____ )`, `____ COUNTY )`) and the MD `STATE OF …, COUNTY OF …, TO WIT:`; notary title lines (`Notary Public ____`, `____, Notary Public`); executed/dated/receipt day-of lines. *Exempt next to a notary cue* (within three lines; a cue is a Notary Public title, a commission/term/appointment/county-of-residence line, a venue line, an MD TO WIT line or a notary seal — never another sentence): the acknowledgment sentences used in OH (incl. statutory `(name of officer)` parentheticals), IN (incl. the SSN-redaction affirmation), MD (`I HEREBY CERTIFY`), MO (`On this … before me appeared`), KS (`This instrument was acknowledged`), WI (`Personally came before me`) and TN, plus `Witness my hand`, `In testimony whereof`, `Given under my hand`; these may run to 900 characters (TN TCA 66-22-107, MD/MO corporate forms), every other line is capped at 400; a bare `STATE/COUNTY OF ____` likewise needs a notary cue. *Exempt next to a signature cue* (a label followed by a blank, `By: ____`, `Name: ____`, or a seal-only line — never prose like `Its lease expires`): party labels (`Grantor`, `Grantee`, `Seller`, `Purchaser`, `Buyer`, `Landlord`, `Tenant`, `Assignor`, `Assignee`, `Return to`, tax-statement lines) on an adjacent line, with nothing after the blank; entity lines (`____, a ____ limited liability company`) and a bare rule in a multi-cell table within three lines (same cell for cell lines). None of these context exemptions applies in a data-captioned exhibit. *Body:* everything else — any line with `$`, a value word after its signature label (rent, rental, rentable, price, parcel, lot, suite, unit, term, deposit, square feet/footage, commencement, expiration, closing, effective, consideration, amount, balance, fee, premises, address, PIN, tax id …), a second non-signature label, or a non-seal parenthetical. Every pattern is anchored and bounded and cue lookup is linear. A heading table's own rows are scanned too. **Form-of exemption with data-caption override** (Gabriel's in-turn rulings, 2026-11-06: Form-of exemption; data captions override it): an exhibit whose caption — the first non-blank block after the heading (spacers, page breaks and a `to Purchase Agreement` heading continuation skipped; soft breaks joined) — reads `Form of X`, `(Form of X)`, `[FORM OF] X`, `X (Form)`, `Exhibit B – Form of X` or `Exhibit B to …: Form of X`, holds no blank, does not say `attached` and has no colon after `Form of`, is a template completed at closing; its blanks are reported as `form_blanks` and never block, until the next block that opens with EXHIBIT/SCHEDULE/ANNEX/APPENDIX/ATTACHMENT/RIDER (whatever follows) or is a short block carrying a data caption — what follows is judged as data. A data caption — rent roll, legal description (`legal descr`), property/premises/land description, any `schedule`, tenant list/roster/schedule, list of leases; and leases, service contracts, permitted exceptions, lease abstract or a personal property list unless a form noun such as assignment is present — is never a form. Blanks on the signature page itself, and in exhibits placed before it, behave as before.
