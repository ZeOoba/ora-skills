# Trade-file close-out checklist

Ten artifacts per engaged trade, per property. Items 3, 4 and 10 are the ones
routinely absent when the file is pulled a year later to make a warranty claim —
which is the moment the file has to be complete.

| # | Artifact | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Conformed, countersigned contract | Signed by the ORA property entity per VC-1, not the manager alone. Both signature pages. |
| 2 | The proposal as received, unmodified | The pre-markup version. Needed to show what changed. |
| 3 | **Certificate of insurance** | Additional insured (Owner + managing agent), primary and non-contributory, waiver of subrogation. Diary the expiry — a COI that lapses mid-job is the same as no COI. |
| 4 | **W-9** | Confirm the legal entity matches the contracting entity. Trade forms frequently contract in a d/b/a. The seed proposal is signed by an agent of *Brothers Standard Striping LLC* trading as two other names. |
| 5 | Scope exhibit / marked aerial | The contractor's own site map, retained as the scope of record. |
| 6 | Pre-work condition photographs | ORA's own, dated, not the contractor's. Establishes what was already failing. |
| 7 | Signed change orders | Per VC-8, Owner's signature in advance. No verbal or deemed change orders in the file. |
| 8 | Punch list and written substantial-completion notice | Per VC-9. Starts the acceptance clock and the warranty term. |
| 9 | Lien waivers | Conditional per progress application; unconditional from contractor, subs and suppliers at final payment (VC-12). |
| 10 | **Warranty certificate with the term and notice period stated** | Extracted onto one page with the substantial-completion date, the warranty expiry, and the defect-notice deadline computed. Diary the expiry 60 days ahead. |

## Warranty diary

The defect-notice period (VC-2) is the term most likely to defeat a valid claim,
and it runs from *discovery*, not from completion. Two calendar entries per
engagement:

- **Warranty expiry minus 60 days** — walk the work with the property manager
  while there is still time to notice a defect.
- **Warranty expiry** — close the file or make the claim.

Register both through `register-scheduled-task` at the time the contract is
countersigned, not at completion.
