docs(package-and-stage-skill): v1.21 - pre-flight frontmatter gate

New step 2a. A description over 1024 chars, or a frontmatter name not
matching its directory, makes the platform importer reject a .skill with
no error text anywhere - the Save-skill click is a silent no-op.
package_skills.py has always caught both, so the gap is only the paths
where it cannot run: cloud Cowork sessions with no DC bridge, hand-built
_staging packages, any .skill assembled without the repo.

been re-imported - and installed a conflicting "v1.20" to the platform
that reverted that size fix. The repo was never written; caught by a
post-hoc repo check. Step 2a's closing paragraph and the changelog now
