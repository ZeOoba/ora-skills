   - Option A: Deploy into next Oxford acquisition (reduces external debt needed, preserves LTV)

   - Option C: Portfolio diversification (add to retirement accounts + brokerage)
7. Impact on Oxford's available personal equity for future deals

**Decision heuristic:** Execute if net proceeds ≥ $100K, rate differential ≤ 1% above current Oxford financing, and redeployment plan with ≥6% return is identified.
