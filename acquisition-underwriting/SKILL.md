Basis + Yield share one denominator — 45 correlated points ride on it. Unverifiable → score carries [est] and blue-cell discipline; say so wherever the score appears.
3. **Dual-brokerage price conflict check (v3.4):** if research surfaces more than one active listing for the same property at different prices, halt Economics scoring (Basis and Yield) and confirm the authorized listing agent and current ask before proceeding. Do NOT average conflicting prices or assume the lower figure is current without direct broker confirmation. Record the discrepancy and source in the QUESTIONS FOR GABRIEL block.
4. **Data Provenance & Conflict Register (v4.0) — extends Rule 3 from price-only to all material fields:** whenever any two sources disagree on a load-bearing field — year built, GBA/office SF, clear height, power, cranes, or any other material physical/vintage fact, not just price — log it to the Data Provenance & Conflict Register (columns: field / OM value / CoStar value / county-auditor value / value used / basis for choice). ## STAGE 1 — FLOORS (flags, not kills — Ruling 5; computed per v1.1-C)

- **Basis floor:** all-in basis (price + 2% closing + CapEx + lease-up carry) discount to replacement, where replacement = **LOW end of the RCT band** (RCT v03: every market is a point ± confidence band — see **Replacement Cost Table (RCT v03)** under UNDERWRITING CONVENTIONS; basis points and floors compute at the LOW end of that market's band). Floor = **linear vintage ramp: 65% at/below 1995 → 60% at/above 2005** (−0.5%/yr). Cite RCT version on every screen. Apply the documented **adder schedule** (heavy office build-out, climate control, cranes, ESFR, excess site work) instead of ad-hoc overrides.
  - ⚠️ **RAMP RECALIBRATED 2026-07-14 (30/25 → 65/60) — THE FLOOR % AND THE DENOMINATOR ARE A MATCHED PAIR.** The old 30/25 ramp was calibrated against RCT v02's *understated* denominator. RCT v03 corrects that denominator (roughly doubling it, per the coherence test). Leaving the ramp at 30/25 against the corrected table would have set the Columbus floor at **$160.76/SF** — a price at which *every* candidate in the buy-box "clears." **A floor that passes everything is not a floor.** The ramp was therefore re-solved to preserve the firm's *revealed* selectivity: ORA's existing clearing prices imply a **mean ~64% discount** to true replacement, not 25–30%. **The 25–30% language was an artifact of a wrong denominator, never a statement of the strategy.** ORA buys at roughly **35–40% of true all-in replacement cost** — say that in IC memos and lender packages; it is a far stronger fact than "a 25% discount."
  - **Net effect of the correction** (v02+30/25 → v03+65/60): **Maryland tightens ~23%** (it had been the most over-generous market) · **Grand Rapids loosens ~16%** and **Indianapolis ~7%** (both had been unfairly harsh) · Columbus, KC and Madison tighten modestly (~3–8%) · Richmond and Des Moines are ~flat. Every move is in the direction the primary-source evidence demands.
- **Renovation effective-vintage rule (Waukee IA audit finding, resolved):** a material renovation shifts the year used for the vintage ramp. Compute renovation spend as a % of that market's RCT v03 POINT replacement cost ("R%"). **R% < 5%** → routine capex, no shift, use the actual year built. **R% ≥ 25%** → full reset, use the renovation-completion year as the effective vintage (treat as newly built for ramp purposes). **5% ≤ R% < 25%** → linear interpolation: effective vintage year = actual year built + [(R% − 5%) ÷ (25% − 5%)] × (renovation year − actual year built). Example: a 1991 building with a 1998 renovation costing 30% of RCT v03 POINT clears the 25% full-reset threshold → effective vintage = 1998. Document R%, the renovation year, and the resulting effective vintage on every screen where this rule is invoked; unverified/asserted renovation spend gets [est] and blue-cell discipline like any other input.
- **DENOMINATOR-SENSITIVE flag (v1.1-C-iii):** any deal within ±5 discount-pts of its floor (at point or low end) OR whose floor verdict flips between point and low end → local replacement verification (insurance comp or contractor quote) REQUIRED pre-LOI.
- **Yield floor:** stabilized YoC >8% on full all-in (prefer 10%+). **Ruling 1: at the walk price, score Yield as-is — 0 pts when the YoC floor binds; no midpoint adjustment.**
- **IRR floor:** levered ≥15% (5-yr) / ≥12% (10-yr); interpolate; risk-adjust up.
- **DSCR floor (per-property governor — v3.8; reported on every UNDERWRITE, not just at refi sizing):** post-refi DSCR must be ≥1.25x at the market-rate refi terms in UNDERWRITING CONVENTIONS (LESSER of 65% LTV per-property and 1.25x DSCR). Report actual computed DSCR alongside the IRR floor line on every screen and underwrite. Breach = flag (Ruling 5), not kill, but state the compensating factor if pursued. This and the IRR floor are the two Stage 1 floors computed with debt layered in; Yield and Basis remain unlevered (see UNDERWRITING CONVENTIONS note). Note: this is the per-property governor — the separate portfolio-aggregate 40% LTV governor is checked at STAGE 2.3, not here.
- **Carrying-cost floor (FLAG, not kill — per Ruling 5):** stabilized total OpEx ≤ **$4.00/SF/yr**, computed on the **pro-forma reassessed property tax at ORA's purchase price** (never the seller's in-place bill) per the State Reassessment Matrix below. Above $4.00/SF → label **CARRY-FLAGGED**; state which component drives the overage and how recovery structure (NNN vs. gross/MTM, vacancy carry) absorbs it. Reassessed tax flows through NOI → the Yield and IRR floors automatically (no double-count).
- **Portfolios:** every building individually clears floors at its **allocated price**, then score the blend. **Allocation method (in priority order):** (1) if credible per-building NOI exists → allocate by **stabilized NOI share**; (2) if NOI is missing or unreliable → allocate by **replacement-cost-weighted GBA** (building SF × RCT v03 POINT for that market ÷ Σ across all buildings); (3) fall back to **raw GBA share** (building SF ÷ portfolio SF) only when construction quality is unknown. State which method was used. A vacant building in an otherwise-leased portfolio is always allocated by method (2) — method (1) would assign it near-zero basis, which is incorrect.
- **Unpriced/no-NOI:** solve walk price to the 8% YoC floor; gap-to-ask = negotiability flag, not a score.
- **Mezzanine (Ruling 2):** partial SF credit ONLY if conditioned + code-compliant egress + leasable; credit % = expected mezz rent ÷ ground-floor rent, **capped at 75%**; otherwise 0% + removal CapEx if dysfunctional.

## STAGE 2 — SCORECARD (100 pts; REPORT AS QUALITY + ECONOMICS + COMBINED per v1.1-A)

**Quality (price-independent, max 55 always)** = Product 20 + Market 12 + Seller 10 + Tenancy 7 + VA 6–13. **Vacant-deal arithmetic (Waukee IA audit finding, resolved):** the prior "62 if vacant" figure was wrong — it double-counted Tenancy's points instead of reallocating them. When 100% vacant, Tenancy scores 0 (no in-place tenant to score) and VA absorbs Tenancy's forfeited 7 pts 1:1 (VA 6 → 13, per the scorecard table row 7). Quality max is **55 in every case** — vacancy shifts points between Tenancy and VA, it never adds points — which is what makes Combined = Quality(55) + Economics(45) = 100 close correctly in both the occupied and vacant case.
**Economics (price-dependent, max 45)** = Basis 25 + Yield 20, at the ORA-actionable price.
**Combined = Quality + Economics** (identical to the v1.0 100-pt score). Unpriced deals rank primarily on Quality; show Economics as a band across the plausible price range.

**Levered IRR reporting (v3.8):** report levered IRR — 5-yr and 10-yr, both pre-tax and after-tax/cost-seg scenarios — alongside Combined and Expected Score on every SCREEN and SNAPSHOT output, not only UNDERWRITE/FULL, so return dispersion is visible for pipeline triage even between deals with similar Combined scores. IRR remains floor-only (Stage 1), NOT point-scored — scorecard weights are unchanged from v1.1. **Clear/loading matrix:** units <3,500 SF → 14'+ clear; 3,500–10,000 → 16'+; >10,000 → 18'+ (higher better). Loading: 1 drive-in per unit <5,000 SF; 1 dock/10,000 SF; more is better. **Seller/Motivation signals (any credible signal scores; stack for more):** distress/life events (estate/probate, divorce, partnership dissolution, lender pressure/REO, tax delinquency, DM fatigue); market evidence (price cuts, DOM >120, failed contracts, broker churn); inexperience (residential/local-only brokerage on a commercial deal, no/bad OM, unpriced, mispriced vs auditor/comps, $0-basis long-hold non-institutional seller).

**Owner-user-vacating (named signal):** an owner-occupant disposing of its OWN now-vacant building is structurally motivated (carrying a non-income building bleeds an operator) and typically mispriced HIGH at listing (anchored to original cost/replacement, not a lease-up buyer's residual), often paired with inexperienced/local brokerage. Scores high in Seller/Motivation; compounds with DOM>120, price cuts, failed contracts.

**Price-watch trigger (owner-user-vacating + basis/price fail):** do NOT cold-PASS. Log the ORA walk price at the basis floor and the implied % gap-to-ask in the Notion deal record. Re-engage when a price cut brings ask within ~5% of ORA walk AND executed/starting rents verify (amendment G). P(exec) rises materially only once the cut lands — move up the Expected-Score pipeline sort at that point, not before. Feeds ora-passed-deal-tracker. Validation: 2196 Port Sheldon — re-engage at ~$1.45–1.50M + verified rents.

Signals also set offer aggressiveness, timing, and P(exec).

**Basis-discount ≠ comp-discount — do not conflate (v3.4):** the Basis score (Stage 1 + scorecard row 1) uses discount-to-RCT-LOW as its input — this measures discount to physical replacement cost of a new building. The Seller/Motivation mispricing signal requires discount-to-executed-resale-comps for the submarket — this measures what buyers actually paid for existing comparable buildings. These are different numbers. A large RCT discount does NOT establish broker mispricing if the resale comp band is near or above the ask; conversely, a modest RCT discount can still reflect mispricing if comps trade at a premium to RCT. Always cite the applicable resale comp band when invoking the mispricing signal; never use RCT as a proxy for comp value.

## STAGE 2.1 — TENANCY BEAR-MODE (v1.1-D; check on EVERY screen)

When market-monitor shows the submarket with **vacancy up >100bps YoY OR two consecutive quarters of negative absorption**: (i) halve Tenancy upside points; (ii) add 12 months of downtime carry to lease-up assumptions (flows into all-in basis). **Report the trigger state (ON/OFF + source + date) on every screen.** **Authoritative source = the `market-monitor` skill** (live vacancy/absorption from CoStar/CBRE/JLL/Colliers quarterly prints). The ranking workbook's Bear-Mode Monitor tab is a **convenience cache only** — if stale (>30 days) or absent, query `market-monitor` directly and cite the live print. On any conflict the live `market-monitor` print wins. **If BOTH sources are unavailable:** record bear-mode state as UNKNOWN, proceed with bear-mode OFF for scoring, but add a flag note that Tenancy upside pts may be overstated if the submarket is distressed — cap the screen at T2 WATCH pending resolution. **Data quality requirement (v3.4):** the vacancy figure used for bear-mode determination must be (a) product-type segmented — industrial/flex vacancy specifically, NOT a blended market average that includes office or speculative bulk distribution — and (b) geography-specific — submarket level preferred; MSA-level industrial accepted only when submarket data is unavailable. Document which level was used. A blended figure inflated by speculative bulk supply is not a valid trigger for shallow-bay/flex ORA targeting. When only a blended figure is available, note "BLENDED — may overstate risk for shallow-bay sub-type" and seek product-specific confirmation before calling bear-mode ON. **Multi-source conflict rule (v3.4):** when two institutional sources disagree on the direction of vacancy change (one shows increase, one shows decrease), apply the more conservative reading.
