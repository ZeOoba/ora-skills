## STAGE -1 — INTAKE RULES (v1.1-G; mandatory before any score is quoted)

**Read `references/doctrine-rationale-and-incidents.md` before disputing or extending any item below.**

1. **County-auditor year-built lookup** for every parcel — the vintage ramp needs a real year; UNK defaults to 1995 (the ramp's oldest endpoint, a **65% discount floor**) and is flagged in QUESTIONS FOR GABRIEL.
2. **Load-bearing rent/NOI verification:** the NOI figure must be verified against a source document (rent roll, lease, income statement) BEFORE any score is quoted. Basis + Yield share one denominator — 45 correlated points ride on it. Unverifiable → score carries [est] and blue-cell discipline; say so wherever the score appears.
3. **Dual-brokerage price conflict check (v3.4):** more than one active listing at different prices → halt Economics scoring (Basis and Yield) until the authorized listing agent and current ask are confirmed. Document which listing carries NOI (no NOI → no Yield score). Never average conflicting prices or assume the lower is current without direct broker confirmation. Record discrepancy + source in QUESTIONS FOR GABRIEL.
4. **Data Provenance & Conflict Register (v4.0):** any two sources disagreeing on a load-bearing field (year built, GBA/office SF, clear height, power, cranes, any material physical/vintage fact — not just price) → log it (field / OM value / CoStar value / county-auditor value / value used / basis for choice). Required on every FULL-mode memo (IC MEMO §3) when a conflict exists; never silently pick one source. Default: county-auditor wins on vintage/legal facts; on-site observation or a signed lease overrides an OM claim; state the basis in every row.
5. **Deal-decision-ledger precedent check (v4.2; SCREEN mode only, before scoring):** read `memory/deal-decision-ledger.md` in full; a candidate sharing geography, asset type, seller-motivation signal, or a named rule with a prior decided row is a precedent hit — cite matching rows by date + deal name in the output scorecard (Stage 2 block). "No precedent rows found" is a valid outcome — say so explicitly.
6. **SF/GBA ingestion-time variance check (v4.6):** diff every disclosed building SF/GBA figure across OM, CoStar and any county/parcel record in hand; variance = |max − min| ÷ min. **≤2%** → proceed. **>2% and ≤5%** → proceed, flag variance + figure used in QUESTIONS FOR GABRIEL and the Conflict Register. **>5%** → **HARD STOP:** halt Basis and Yield scoring, cap the verdict at **T2 WATCH**, resolve (county record, survey, or direct broker confirmation) before Combined is finalized. Distinct from STAGE 2.6 item 3 (LOI-stage, >2%).
7. **Scanned / no-text-layer OM check (v4.7):** run `pdfinfo` on every uploaded OM before extraction. Producer "Microsoft: Print To PDF" (or any rasterizer), or empty `pdftotext` → **no text layer**: extract by page-image render (`pdftoppm -png -r 110`) and visual read, page by page — a failed text pull is NEVER evidence of an empty document. Tag every such figure `[OM-visual]` and reconcile per Rule 4.
8. **Zoning district + current assessed value at intake (v4.14):** record for every parcel (i) the **zoning district from the municipality's live zoning GIS layer** (never the assessor's field) via `ora-flood-zoning-dd`, and (ii) the **current assessed value and tax bill on the roll** via `ora-millage-lookup`. Catches (a) an industrial building in a **commercial/mixed-use district** — legal nonconforming, with a 12-month (WI/OH/IN) discontinuance clock that turns re-letting into re-entitlement, a basis-level fact; (b) an **anomalous assessment** (exempt coding, $0 value, stale/absent record) that invalidates any trended pro-forma tax and can carry retroactive omitted-property exposure. Either caps the verdict at **T2 WATCH** until resolved and goes in QUESTIONS FOR GABRIEL. Fires on every mode.
9. **Ancillary-income lease abstraction (v4.30).** For every cell-tower, billboard, solar, rooftop-antenna or easement agreement, record: premises area (the legal-description figure governs over a body-text approximation); rent commencement and whether it has started; initial term **and every renewal option** (count × length, automatic or at tenant election); escalator; **termination rights** (who, notice, fee, and whether the right is at will); ROFR/ROFO on a sale of the fee or the rent stream; relocation rights; and assignment/estoppel mechanics. Model the **full contractual stream**, not a 10-year schedule. Where the tenant can terminate at will, carry a termination case in the Bear scenario and say so. A rent stream carved out of the sale ("towers sold separately") is removed from NOI and logged in the Conflict Register (Rule 4). Origin: 8625 Troost Ave (2026-10-17). The AT&T Option & Land Lease runs 5 + 17 × 5 years with +10% per term and is terminable on 60 days plus three months' rent, but the model's Inputs schedule stopped at 2037.

## STAGE 0 — GATES (Ruling 5: NO HARD KILLS — breaches are FLAGS, screened as exceptions; only Gabriel kills a deal)

A gate breach = prominent flag + screen/score as exception anyway; verdict label **GATE-FLAGGED** (never KILLED). Pure out-of-market junk gets flagged-and-deprioritized rather than scored in depth. **Institutional benchmark note (v4.3):** banks (OCC Handbook) hard-gate environmental, structural and zoning defects; Ruling 5's flag-not-kill is a **deliberate, correct divergence** that lets an all-cash generational-hold family office look at deals a bank must auto-reject — never let an "institutional hardening" pass walk it back.

1. **Product:** Class B/C industrial — shallow-bay multi-tenant, flex, office-warehouse, OR functional single-tenant ≤40,000 SF (scored EQUAL to multi-tenant). Quasi-IOS single-tenant <20K SF on land-heavy parcels = explicit target profile. Office % never kills if 1-story with adequate clear behind the finish (demo-able); ≤30% ideal.
2. **Size/Price:** $500K–$10M single asset; ≤$15M multi-building portfolio; <50,000 SF per building.
3. **Market:** Established clusters (Columbus, Cincinnati, Kansas City, Indianapolis, Maryland/DC, Madison WI) + entry clusters (Grand Rapids, Richmond, Des Moines, **Louisville KY**, **Chicago–Greater Cook County IL**). Satellites: ONLY within 30 min of an ESTABLISHED cluster, Market pts capped at half. **Ruling 3: entry clusters earn NO satellite eligibility until ORA's first closed deal validates the cluster.** Louisville KY = 10th canonical cluster (`target-markets.json` v1.7) — ENTRY, not established. **Chicago–Greater Cook County IL = 11th canonical cluster (`target-markets.json` v1.11, ratified 2026-09-29) — ENTRY; scope = all of Cook County (City of Chicago + suburban Cook); DuPage/Will/Lake/Kane/McHenry are NOT satellites until a first Cook close (Ruling 3). Cook's property-tax regime is the market's defining risk — every Cook screen runs the IL/Cook reassessment row.** **OBSOLESCENCE-WATCH (v4.3, reported not scored):** Columbus and Indianapolis are low-barrier, elevated-obsolescence markets (Prologis) — **it cuts both ways** (competing subdivided bulk supply vs. bulk stock migrating to light-industrial use); flag both sides in the pre-mortem for any deal there near the vintage-ramp floor.
4. **Physical:** multi-story warehouse; no ground-level access (zero drive-ins AND zero docks); clear height <12'.
5. **Flood:** SFHA on building pad = gate flag. SFHA on excess land = land at $0, proceed. **Ruling 4: land credit = $0 in the basis test until FEMA determination lands; report the verified-dry score as an upside band.**
6. **Other:** ground lease <30 yrs; remediation >20% of price; automotive use; child-use without safety review; retail-only; office-only. **Automotive, retail-only and office-only additionally trigger STAGE 2.9 (Alternative Asset Class Track, v4.6)** alongside standard scoring — the industrial-calibrated Basis/Yield floors remain the governing test; a parallel land/redevelopment-value read is warranted.

## STAGE 1 — FLOORS (flags, not kills — Ruling 5; computed per v1.1-C)

- **Basis floor:** all-in basis (price + 2% closing + CapEx + lease-up carry) discount to replacement, where replacement = **LOW end of the RCT band** (RCT v03: every market is a point ± confidence band — see **Replacement Cost Table (RCT v03)** under UNDERWRITING CONVENTIONS; basis points and floors compute at the LOW end of that market's band). Floor = **linear vintage ramp: 65% at/below 1995 → 60% at/above 2005** (−0.5%/yr). Cite RCT version on every screen. Apply the documented **adder schedule** (heavy office build-out, climate control, cranes, ESFR, excess site work, paving/lighting intensity) instead of ad-hoc overrides.
  - ⚠️ **RAMP RECALIBRATED 2026-07-14 (30/25 → 65/60) — THE FLOOR % AND THE DENOMINATOR ARE A MATCHED PAIR** (the 30/25 ramp belonged to RCT v02's understated denominator). **ORA buys at roughly 35–40% of true all-in replacement cost — say that in IC memos and lender packages**, never "a 25% discount" (a wrong-denominator artifact).
- **Renovation effective-vintage rule:** renovation spend as % of the market's RCT v03 POINT ("R%"). **R% < 5%** → no shift. **R% ≥ 25%** → full reset to the renovation-completion year. **5% ≤ R% < 25%** → effective vintage = actual year built + [(R% − 5%) ÷ 20%] × (renovation year − actual year built). Document R%, renovation year and effective vintage on every screen invoking it; asserted spend gets [est] and blue-cell discipline.
- **DENOMINATOR-SENSITIVE flag (v1.1-C-iii):** any deal within ±5 discount-pts of its floor (at point or low end) OR whose floor verdict flips between point and low end → local replacement verification (insurance comp or contractor quote) REQUIRED pre-LOI.
- **Yield floor:** stabilized YoC >8% on full all-in (prefer 10%+). **Ruling 1: at the walk price, score Yield as-is — 0 pts when the YoC floor binds; no midpoint adjustment.**
- **IRR floor:** levered ≥15% (5-yr) / ≥12% (10-yr); interpolate; risk-adjust up.
- **DSCR floor (per-property governor — v3.8; reported on every UNDERWRITE, not just at refi sizing):** post-refi DSCR must be ≥1.25x at the market-rate refi terms in UNDERWRITING CONVENTIONS (LESSER of 65% LTV per-property and 1.25x DSCR). Report computed DSCR alongside the IRR floor line on every screen and underwrite. Breach = flag (Ruling 5), not kill; state the compensating factor if pursued. DSCR and IRR are the two floors computed with debt layered in; Yield and Basis remain unlevered. The portfolio-aggregate 40% LTV governor is checked at STAGE 2.3, not here.
- **Carrying-cost floor (FLAG, not kill — per Ruling 5):** stabilized total OpEx ≤ **$4.00/SF/yr**, computed on the **pro-forma reassessed property tax at ORA's purchase price** (never the seller's in-place bill) per the State Reassessment Matrix (`references/state-reassessment-matrix.md`). Above $4.00/SF → label **CARRY-FLAGGED**; state which component drives the overage and how recovery structure (NNN vs. gross/MTM, vacancy carry) absorbs it. Reassessed tax flows through NOI → the Yield and IRR floors automatically (no double-count).
- **Portfolios:** every building individually clears floors at its **allocated price**, then score the blend. Allocation priority: (1) credible per-building NOI → **stabilized NOI share**; (2) otherwise **replacement-cost-weighted GBA** (building SF × RCT v03 POINT ÷ Σ); (3) **raw GBA share** only when construction quality is unknown. State the method; a vacant building in a leased portfolio is always allocated by (2).
- **Priced, no-NOI:** Yield = 0 (documented no-NOI convention — never null); Basis scores normally at the scoring price (ORA's walk price, else the disclosed price) — never a YoC-solved price.
- **Unpriced, NOI known:** solve walk price to the 8% YoC floor using the disclosed NOI; gap-to-ask = negotiability flag, not a score.
- **Unpriced AND no-NOI (v4.21):** do NOT solve a walk price from an externally-sourced or linked-record implied NOI (e.g. a pipeline row's Purchase Price × Cap Rate) — that imports an assumption the frozen-facts SCREEN discipline excludes. Yield = 0 and **Basis = null** with a `[DATA-GAP: no price, no NOI]` flag. A broker/pipeline-implied cap rate may appear only as a labeled SENSITIVITY BAND, never as the scored Basis input.
- **Mezzanine (Ruling 2):** partial SF credit ONLY if conditioned + code-compliant egress + leasable; credit % = expected mezz rent ÷ ground-floor rent, **capped at 75%**; otherwise 0% + removal CapEx if dysfunctional.

## STAGE 2 — SCORECARD (100 pts; REPORT AS QUALITY + ECONOMICS + COMBINED per v1.1-A)

**Quality (price-independent, max 55 always)** = Product 20 + Market 12 + Seller 10 + Tenancy 7 + VA 6–13. **Vacant-deal arithmetic:** 100% vacant → Tenancy 0, VA absorbs the forfeited 7 pts 1:1 (VA 6 → 13). Quality max is **55 in every case** (the retired "62 if vacant" double-counted), so Combined = 55 + 45 = 100 closes in both cases.
**Economics (price-dependent, max 45)** = Basis 25 + Yield 20 at the ORA-actionable price.
**Combined = Quality + Economics**. Unpriced deals rank primarily on Quality; show Economics as a band across the plausible price range.

**Levered IRR reporting (v3.8; after-tax wording v4.26):** report levered IRR — 5-yr and 10-yr, pre-tax and after-tax (federal and federal + state, per the After-Tax Step / `cost-seg-tax-layer`) — alongside Combined and Expected Score on every SCREEN and SNAPSHOT output. On SCREEN/SNAPSHOT the after-tax line is a one-line `[EST]` using the skill's default allocation case and the address state; the full After-Tax Step runs in FULL. Every after-tax figure names its allocation case and state. IRR remains floor-only, NOT point-scored; converting it is reserved for the December back-test — never hand-adjust weights mid-cycle.

**Batch-ranking compression flag — Yield = 0 (v3.4):** when >50% of a ranked batch has Yield = 0 (no NOI), the ranking is Quality + Basis only — flag this at the top of the output; rank within a ≤5-pt Combined band is below estimation precision. Prioritize NOI-disclosed deals for broker call time; call out any deal a disclosed yield would materially move.

**Clear/loading matrix:** units <3,500 SF → 14'+ clear; 3,500–10,000 → 16'+; >10,000 → 18'+. Loading: 1 drive-in per unit <5,000 SF; 1 dock/10,000 SF; more is better. Truck court 100' ideal; <60' ≈ 0 site pts (no kill). Construction: block/tilt-up/brick full credit; PEMB ≈ half, never kills.

**Seller/Motivation signals (any credible signal scores; stack for more):** distress/life events (estate/probate, divorce, partnership dissolution, lender pressure/REO, tax delinquency, DM fatigue); market evidence (price cuts, DOM >120, failed contracts, broker churn); inexperience (residential/local-only brokerage, no/bad OM, unpriced, mispriced vs auditor/comps, $0-basis long-hold non-institutional seller). **Owner-user-vacating (named signal):** an owner-occupant disposing of its OWN now-vacant building is structurally motivated and typically mispriced HIGH (anchored to cost/replacement, not a lease-up residual) — scores high; compounds with DOM>120, price cuts, failed contracts. Signals also set offer aggressiveness, timing, and P(exec).

**Price-watch trigger (owner-user-vacating + basis/price fail):** do NOT cold-PASS. Log the ORA walk price at the basis floor and the % gap-to-ask in the Notion deal record; re-engage when a price cut brings ask within ~5% of ORA walk AND executed/starting rents verify (amendment G) — P(exec) moves only once the cut lands. Feeds ora-passed-deal-tracker.

**Basis-discount ≠ comp-discount — do not conflate (v3.4):** the Basis score uses discount-to-RCT-LOW (replacement cost of a new building); the Seller/Motivation mispricing signal requires discount-to-executed-resale-comps. A large RCT discount does NOT establish mispricing if the comp band is near or above the ask, and vice versa. Always cite the resale comp band when invoking the mispricing signal; never use RCT as a proxy for comp value. **IOS return-claim caution (v4.3):** operator/broker-reported IOS return figures (8%+ untrended yields, 20%+ IRRs, "2x rent growth") are marketing, not comparable data — never let them inflate VA confidence beyond the deal's own verified numbers.

**Environmental:** flag + price (Phase I always; Phase II OK with cap; >20% of price = gate flag). NOT scored. **Phase II probability escalators (v3.4)** — any confirmed at screen → "Phase II HIGH probability", priced before LOI: (i) oil-water separator or grease trap; (ii) active UST registry hit (IDEM, MDEP, WDNR, or equivalent); (iii) in-use floor drains in a service, wash or manufacturing bay; (iv) automotive, petroleum, dry-cleaning or industrial-solvent SIC tenancy (current or prior); (v) adjacent parcel with confirmed LUST or active plume. Not a kill (Ruling 5), but requires an explicit cost-range estimate and compensating factor before advancing past SCREEN; sets verdict flag **ENV-WATCH** (v3.8) until both are logged.

## STAGE 2.1 — TENANCY BEAR-MODE (v1.1-D; check on EVERY screen)

**Trigger:** submarket **vacancy up >100bps YoY OR two consecutive quarters of negative absorption** (per market-monitor) → (i) halve Tenancy upside points; (ii) add 12 months of downtime carry to lease-up assumptions (flows into all-in basis). **Report the trigger state (ON/OFF + source + date) on every screen.**

- **Authoritative source = the `market-monitor` skill** (live CoStar/CBRE/JLL/Colliers prints). The ranking workbook's Bear-Mode Monitor tab is a **convenience cache only** — stale (>30 days) or absent → query `market-monitor`; on conflict the live print wins.
- **If BOTH sources are unavailable:** record bear-mode state as UNKNOWN, proceed with bear-mode OFF for scoring, but add a flag note that Tenancy upside pts may be overstated if the submarket is distressed — cap the screen at T2 WATCH pending resolution.
- **Data quality (v3.4):** the vacancy figure must be (a) product-segmented — industrial/flex, NOT a blended average with office or speculative bulk — and (b) geography-specific — submarket preferred; MSA-level industrial only when submarket data is unavailable. Document the level used; blended-only → note "BLENDED — may overstate risk for shallow-bay sub-type" and seek product-specific confirmation before calling bear-mode ON.
- **Vendor/operator "state of the market" reports** (co-warehousing operators, brokerage lead-gen, any self-published report with a promotional interest) are DIRECTIONAL-ONLY — never a bear-mode trigger, never an exit-cap input; macro color may be cited in narrative, tagged by source and date.
- **Chicago–Greater Cook County (v4.25):** `market-monitor` v1.6 carries the Cook profile (CoStar Near South Cook baseline, BLENDED — a baseline, not a live print); for bear-mode source a live CoStar or C&W MarketBeat Chicago submarket print (product-segmented where available; tag BLENDED otherwise) and cap the screen at T2 WATCH if neither is available.
