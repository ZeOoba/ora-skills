## STAGE 1 — FLOORS (flags, not kills — Ruling 5; computed per v1.1-C)

- **Basis floor:** all-in basis (price + 2% closing + CapEx + lease-up carry) discount to replacement, where replacement = **LOW end of the RCT band** (RCT v02: every market is a point ± confidence band — see **Replacement Cost Table (RCT v02)** under UNDERWRITING CONVENTIONS; basis points and floors compute at the LOW end of that market's band). Floor = **linear vintage ramp: 30% at/below 1995 → 25% at/above 2005** (−0.5%/yr; replaces the 25/30 cliff). Cite RCT version on every screen. Apply the documented **adder schedule** (heavy office build-out, climate control, cranes, ESFR, excess site work) instead of ad-hoc overrides.
- **Renovation effective-vintage rule (Waukee IA audit finding, resolved):** a material renovation shifts the year used for the vintage ramp. Compute renovation spend as a % of that market's RCT v02 POINT replacement cost ("R%"). **R% < 5%** → routine capex, no shift, use the actual year built. **R% ≥ 25%** → full reset, use the renovation-completion year as the effective vintage (treat as newly built for ramp purposes). **5% ≤ R% < 25%** → linear interpolation: effective vintage year = actual year built + [(R% − 5%) ÷ (25% − 5%)] × (renovation year − actual year built). Example: a 1991 building with a 1998 renovation costing 30% of RCT POINT clears the 25% full-reset threshold → effective vintage = 1998. Document R%, the renovation year, and the resulting effective vintage on every screen where this rule is invoked; unverified/asserted renovation spend gets [est] and blue-cell discipline like any other input.
- **DENOMINATOR-SENSITIVE flag (v1.1-C-iii):** any deal within ±5 discount-pts of its floor (at point or low end) OR whose floor verdict flips between point and low end → local replacement verification (insurance comp or contractor quote) REQUIRED pre-LOI.
- **Yield floor:** stabilized YoC >8% on full all-in (prefer 10%+). **Ruling 1: at the walk price, score Yield as-is — 0 pts when the YoC floor binds; no midpoint adjustment.**
- **IRR floor:** levered ≥15% (5-yr) / ≥12% (10-yr); interpolate; risk-adjust up.
- **DSCR floor (per-property governor — v3.8; reported on every UNDERWRITE, not just at refi sizing):** post-refi DSCR must be ≥1.25x at the market-rate refi terms in UNDERWRITING CONVENTIONS (LESSER of 65% LTV per-property and 1.25x DSCR). Report actual computed DSCR alongside the IRR floor line on every screen and underwrite. Breach = flag (Ruling 5), not kill, but state the compensating factor if pursued. This and the IRR floor are the two Stage 1 floors computed with debt layered in; Yield and Basis remain unlevered (see UNDERWRITING CONVENTIONS note). Note: this is the per-property governor — the separate portfolio-aggregate 40% LTV governor is checked at STAGE 2.3, not here.
- **Carrying-cost floor (FLAG, not kill — per Ruling 5):** stabilized total OpEx ≤ **$4.00/SF/yr**, computed on the **pro-forma reassessed property tax at ORA's purchase price** (never the seller's in-place bill) per the State Reassessment Matrix below. Above $4.00/SF → label **CARRY-FLAGGED**; state which component drives the overage and how recovery structure (NNN vs. gross/MTM, vacancy carry) absorbs it. Reassessed tax flows through NOI → the Yield and IRR floors automatically (no double-count).
- **Portfolios:** every building individually clears floors at its **allocated price**, then score the blend. **Allocation method (in priority order):** (1) if credible per-building NOI exists → allocate by **stabilized NOI share**; (2) if NOI is missing or unreliable → allocate by **replacement-cost-weighted GBA** (building SF × RCT v02 POINT for that market ÷ Σ across all buildings); (3) fall back to **raw GBA share** (building SF ÷ portfolio SF) only when construction quality is unknown. State which method was used. A vacant building in an otherwise-leased portfolio is always allocated by method (2) — method (1) would assign it near-zero basis, which is incorrect.
- **Unpriced/no-NOI:** solve walk price to the 8% YoC floor; gap-to-ask = negotiability flag, not a score.
- **Mezzanine (Ruling 2):** partial SF credit ONLY if conditioned + code-compliant egress + leasable; credit % = expected mezz rent ÷ ground-floor rent, **capped at 75%**; otherwise 0% + removal CapEx if dysfunctional.

## STAGE 2 — SCORECARD (100 pts; REPORT AS QUALITY + ECONOMICS + COMBINED per v1.1-A)

**Quality (price-independent, max 55 always)** = Product 20 + Market 12 + Seller 10 + Tenancy 7 + VA 6–13. **Vacant-deal arithmetic (Waukee IA audit finding, resolved):** the prior "62 if vacant" figure was wrong — it double-counted Tenancy's points instead of reallocating them. When 100% vacant, Tenancy scores 0 (no in-place tenant to score) and VA absorbs Tenancy's forfeited 7 pts 1:1 (VA 6 → 13, per the scorecard table row 7). Quality max is **55 in every case** — vacancy shifts points between Tenancy and VA, it never adds points — which is what makes Combined = Quality(55) + Economics(45) = 100 close correctly in both the occupied and vacant case.
**Economics (price-dependent, max 45)** = Basis 25 + Yield 20, at the ORA-actionable price.
**Combined = Quality + Economics** (identical to the v1.0 100-pt score). Unpriced deals rank primarily on Quality; show Economics as a band across the plausible price range.

**Levered IRR reporting (v3.8):** report levered IRR — 5-yr and 10-yr, both pre-tax and after-tax/cost-seg scenarios — alongside Combined and Expected Score on every SCREEN and SNAPSHOT output, not only UNDERWRITE/FULL, so return dispersion is visible for pipeline triage even between deals with similar Combined scores. IRR remains floor-only (Stage 1), NOT point-scored — scorecard weights are unchanged from v1.1. Converting IRR from floor to a scored Economics component is a calibration-governed decision reserved for the annual December back-test (Outcome Feedback, v1.1-E); do not hand-adjust weights mid-cycle.

**Batch-ranking compression flag — Yield = 0 (v3.4):** when >50% of deals in a ranked batch have Yield = 0 (no NOI disclosed), the ranking is driven by Quality + Basis only — not a full 100-pt scorecard. Flag this explicitly at the top of any batch-ranking output. Relative rank within a ≤5-pt Combined band is below estimation precision when Yield is uniformly zero. Prioritize deals with NOI disclosure for broker call time; call out any deal where a disclosed yield would materially move rank.

**Clear/loading matrix:** units <3,500 SF → 14'+ clear; 3,500–10,000 → 16'+; >10,000 → 18'+ (higher better). Loading: 1 drive-in per unit <5,000 SF; 1 dock/10,000 SF; more is better. Truck court 100' ideal; <60' ≈ 0 site pts (no kill). Construction: block/tilt-up/brick full credit; PEMB ≈ half, never kills.

**Seller/Motivation signals (any credible signal scores; stack for more):** distress/life events (estate/probate, divorce, partnership dissolution, lender pressure/REO, tax delinquency, DM fatigue); market evidence (price cuts, DOM >120, failed contracts, broker churn); inexperience (residential/local-only brokerage on a commercial deal, no/bad OM, unpriced, mispriced vs auditor/comps, $0-basis long-hold non-institutional seller).

**Owner-user-vacating (named signal):** an owner-occupant disposing of its OWN now-vacant building is structurally motivated (carrying a non-income building bleeds an operator) and typically mispriced HIGH at listing (anchored to original cost/replacement, not a lease-up buyer's residual), often paired with inexperienced/local brokerage. Scores high in Seller/Motivation; compounds with DOM>120, price cuts, failed contracts.

**Price-watch trigger (owner-user-vacating + basis/price fail):** do NOT cold-PASS. Log the ORA walk price at the basis floor and the implied % gap-to-ask in the Notion deal record. Re-engage when a price cut brings ask within ~5% of ORA walk AND executed/starting rents verify (amendment G). P(exec) rises materially only once the cut lands — move up the Expected-Score pipeline sort at that point, not before. Feeds ora-passed-deal-tracker. Validation: 2196 REDACTED-119 — re-engage at ~$1.45–1.50M + verified rents.

Signals also set offer aggressiveness, timing, and P(exec).

**Basis-discount ≠ comp-discount — do not conflate (v3.4):** the Basis score (Stage 1 + scorecard row 1) uses discount-to-RCT-LOW as its input — this measures discount to physical replacement cost of a new building. The Seller/Motivation mispricing signal requires discount-to-executed-resale-comps for the submarket — this measures what buyers actually paid for existing comparable buildings. These are different numbers. A large RCT discount does NOT establish broker mispricing if the resale comp band is near or above the ask; conversely, a modest RCT discount can still reflect mispricing if comps trade at a premium to RCT. Always cite the applicable resale comp band when invoking the mispricing signal; never use RCT as a proxy for comp value.

**Environmental:** flag + price (Phase I always; Phase II OK with cap; >20% of price = gate flag). NOT scored. **Phase II probability escalators (v3.4):** when any of the following are confirmed at screen, upgrade to "Phase II HIGH probability" and price accordingly before LOI — do not wait for Phase I to trigger it: (i) oil-water separator (OWS) or grease trap on-site; (ii) active UST registry hit (IDEM, MDEP, WDNR, or equivalent state DB); (iii) in-use floor drains in a service bay, wash bay, or manufacturing area; (iv) automotive, petroleum-handling, dry-cleaning, or industrial-solvent SIC tenancy (current or prior); (v) adjacent parcel with confirmed LUST or active plume (subject is a potential receptor). Phase II HIGH probability is not a gate kill (Ruling 5) but requires explicit environmental cost-range estimate and a compensating factor before advancing past SCREEN. **Verdict flag (v3.8):** Phase II HIGH probability sets verdict flag **ENV-WATCH**, displayed in the verdict block alongside CARRY-FLAGGED / DENOMINATOR-SENSITIVE / CONCENTRATION-FLAGGED and carried into VERDICT BANDS reporting until the cost-range estimate and compensating factor are logged.

## STAGE 2.1 — TENANCY BEAR-MODE (v1.1-D; check on EVERY screen)

When market-monitor shows the submarket with **vacancy up >100bps YoY OR two consecutive quarters of negative absorption**: (i) halve Tenancy upside points; (ii) add 12 months of downtime carry to lease-up assumptions (flows into all-in basis). **Report the trigger state (ON/OFF + source + date) on every screen.** **Authoritative source = the `market-monitor` skill** (live vacancy/absorption from CoStar/CBRE/JLL/Colliers quarterly prints). The ranking workbook's Bear-Mode Monitor tab is a **convenience cache only** — if stale (>30 days) or absent, query `market-monitor` directly and cite the live print. On any conflict the live `market-monitor` print wins. **If BOTH sources are unavailable:** record bear-mode state as UNKNOWN, proceed with bear-mode OFF for scoring, but add a flag note that Tenancy upside pts may be overstated if the submarket is distressed — cap the screen at T2 WATCH pending resolution. **Data quality requirement (v3.4):** the vacancy figure used for bear-mode determination must be (a) product-type segmented — industrial/flex vacancy specifically, NOT a blended market average that includes office or speculative bulk distribution — and (b) geography-specific — submarket level preferred; MSA-level industrial accepted only when submarket data is unavailable. Document which level was used. A blended figure inflated by speculative bulk supply is not a valid trigger for shallow-bay/flex ORA targeting. When only a blended figure is available, note "BLENDED — may overstate risk for shallow-bay sub-type" and seek product-specific confirmation before calling bear-mode ON. **Multi-source conflict rule (v3.4):** when two institutional sources disagree on the direction of vacancy change (one shows increase, one shows decrease), apply the more conservative reading. Bear-mode is called OFF only when BOTH sources fall below their respective trigger thresholds. If sources conflict on direction, record bear-mode state as CONFLICTED — treat identically to UNKNOWN (score as OFF; cap at T2 WATCH; require resolution before LOI). **Grand Rapids MI standing caution (June 2026 deep research):** net absorption reversed to −854K SF YTD Q4 2025 (from +408K SF in 2024); Michigan manufacturing shed −2,500 jobs April–November 2025 — always verify Grand Rapids bear-mode state with `market-monitor` before any screen; do NOT default to OFF based on headline 3.7% vacancy, which predates the absorption reversal.

## STAGE 2.2 — P(EXEC) + EXPECTED SCORE (v1.1-B; pipeline sort)

Assign P(execution) from already-collected seller signals. These are the **operative default bands** — apply on every screen. ## STAGE 2.3 — PORTFOLIO CONCENTRATION CHECK (v3.8; run on every SCREEN and FULL)

## STAGE 2.5 — DD-CRITICAL SCREEN ITEMS (five base items + one condo-conditional; any unknown caps verdict at T2 WATCH)

**"Capped at T2" disambiguation (Waukee IA audit finding, resolved):** "capped at T2 WATCH" is a CEILING, not a floor. It blocks a deal from reaching **T1 PURSUE** while any DD-Critical item is unknown — it does NOT force a deal that raw-scores in PASS-band (<60) up to WATCH. A raw score of 45 with an unresolved DD-Critical item is still reported PASS-band with the DD-Critical gap noted; a raw score of 80 with an unresolved DD-Critical item is reported T2 WATCH (ceiling applied), not T1 PURSUE. This is the same ceiling semantics already used for bear-mode UNKNOWN/CONFLICTED (Stage 2.1: "cap the screen at T2 WATCH pending resolution" while scoring bear-mode OFF) — the two rules are now explicitly aligned, not independently worded.

1. **FEMA flood determination** — NFHL query (use ora-flood-zoning-dd skill; NFHL REST unreliable → county/state floodplain GIS or browser MSC fallback). Never assert flood status without a source.
2. **Zoning designation + permitted-use check** — municipal GIS/code at screen; formal zoning letter still required pre-LOI.
3. **Roof age/type/remaining life** — [est] OK with stated basis; replacement CapEx priced into all-in basis.
4. **Deferred-maintenance CapEx estimate** — [est] OK; priced into all-in basis.
5. **Pro-forma reassessed property tax + total carrying cost ($/SF)** — compute taxes on the post-sale reassessed basis via the State Reassessment Matrix (assessment ratio × market/price basis × effective millage, net of in-place abatement/PILOT and applicable caps — IN 3% GAV, IA 90% rollback, MD 3-yr phase-in).
