**v4.4 (2026-07-23):** Added **STAGE 2.8 — Functional Obsolescence & Durability Grade (FORG v1.0, PROPOSED)**, inserted between Stage 2.7 and Stage 3. Runs on every FULL; recommended-not-mandatory on SCREEN when Stage 2.5/2.7 flags material vintage, vacancy, or land-heavy characteristics. Produces two independent 0–100 scores — **Building Durability Score (BDS**, 8 factors) and **Land & Locational Offset Score (LOS**, 5 factors) — resolved through a 3×3 band matrix into a **Functional Obsolescence Risk Tier (FORT, A–D)**, reported in the verdict block alongside CARRY-FLAGGED / ENV-WATCH / LTV-FLAGGED / OBSOLESCENCE-WATCH. **FORT is deliberately NOT folded into Combined** — it is a durability/hold-horizon read, structurally distinct from the price-dependent Economics score and the current-condition Product & Physical row. *Why now:* the skill previously scored a building's condition and its price, but had no instrument for the question that actually governs a generational hold — how fast does this specific building, for its specific realistic tenant pool, age out of usefulness, and how much does the land underneath it offset that. Part A's tenant-class gate is the load-bearing piece: [PRIMARY] Prologis Research (2015/updated 2025-12-07) finds obsolescence pace is tenant-type-dependent, and ORA's buy-box sits in the slowest-obsolescing tier (light-industrial/local-trade) by construction — so scoring ORA product against a national/international bulk spec standard would systematically and wrongly condemn it. Also adds one genuinely new DD item (electrical service size + utility-feed capacity, resolving BDS-4's [SPECULATIVE] tag deal-by-deal) and generalizes the existing v4.3 OBSOLESCENCE-WATCH flag from a two-market callout into a scored LOS factor across all markets, retaining its two-sided risk/tailwind framing. Stage 2.8 is **excluded from the FULL-mode parallel fan-out** (MODES/FULL) because LOS-3 imports Stage 2.7's Acquisition Location Score as an input — same sequencing rationale that already excludes Stage 2.6. **Weights are PROPOSED, not ratified:** pilot manually on the next 3–5 SCREEN-stage candidates with the FORT tier logged to the Deal Sourcing Log before any BDS/LOS point weight is treated as binding — the same discipline the annual December back-test imposes on every other weight change.
