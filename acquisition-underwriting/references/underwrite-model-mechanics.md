## After-tax reproducibility convention (full text, from UNDERWRITING CONVENTIONS "Cost segregation + 100% bonus")

**After-tax reproducibility convention (v4.0):** the FEDERAL depreciation/recapture mechanics are specified precisely enough to be deterministic and MUST be modeled identically run-to-run: (i) 15% of building basis to ≤15-yr MACRS personal-property life, 100% bonus depreciation Year 1; (ii) the remaining 85% of building basis on straight-line 39-yr real property, mid-month MACRS convention; (iii) on a taxable sale — Section 1245 ordinary recapture on the cost-segged/bonus-depreciated portion, taxed at ORDINARY income rates with **NO** 25% cap (the IRC §1(h) 25% ceiling applies only to unrecaptured Section 1250 gain, never to §1245 — model §1245 at ORA's standing conservative top-bracket assumption of 37% federal, same "modeling convention, not Gabriel's actual bracket" caveat as the LTCG figure below); unrecaptured Section 1250 gain on the straight-line portion at 25% federal (the actual §1(h) cap); remaining gain at ORA's standing conservative assumption of 20% federal LTCG + 3.8% NIIT (a modeling convention, not Gabriel's actual bracket — flag as such); (iv) on a §1031 exchange — ALL recapture and gain recognition above is deferred via carryover basis into the replacement property; current-year tax on the exchanged gain = $0. **State overlay + the final bottom-line dollar figure stay `[EST] — illustrative until CPA input provided`:** ORA's footprint spans 8 states (MI/OH/IN/MD/MO/WI/KS/TN) with materially different, frequently-changing individual/entity income-tax treatment, and the true after-tax number also depends on Gabriel's/the holding entity's actual marginal bracket and other income in the year of sale — facts this property-level model does not and should not assume. Report the deterministic FEDERAL-mechanics after-tax IRR as the reproducible standing figure; caveat the state-tax-adjusted bottom line as illustrative pending CPA confirmation on every IC memo and UNDERWRITE. **Reasoning:** the depreciation/recapture MATH is IRC-defined and stable — fixing it deterministically closes the "won't reproduce run-to-run" gap the audit flagged. State tax rates and Gabriel's personal/entity bracket are not stable inputs to this skill — hard-coding them risks stating stale or wrong figures as doctrine (a fabrication risk under Hard Invariant 7), so those stay flagged illustrative rather than fixed.

## Worked MI reassessment example (full text, from UNDERWRITING CONVENTIONS)

**Worked MI example (v4.0 — mechanics reference for the OpEx & Carrying Cost tab's reassessment mini-calculator; real validation-deal figures, not synthetic):** subject 2900 Northridge Dr NW, Walker MI — purchase price $2,250,000, 22,600 SF. MI resets SEV to ~50% of the transfer price the year after sale, and Taxable Value uncaps to match SEV in the transfer year (MCL 211.27a(6)(h)): pro-forma SEV = 50% × $2,250,000 = $1,125,000 = pro-forma Taxable Value (no cap survives a sale). Millage — **`[EST, but DERIVED not guessed: this parcel's own disclosed 2025 tax bill ($25,178.65) ÷ its 2025 Taxable Value ($562,626) = 44.75 mills; confirm against the City of Walker/Kent County treasurer for the transfer year, since millage can shift year to year]`**: reassessed annual tax = $1,125,000 × 0.04475 = **$50,344/yr** ÷ 22,600 SF = **$2.23/SF/yr** in reassessed property tax alone — matching, almost to the dollar, the hand-typed `[EST] ~$50,300/yr` narrative figure the pre-v4.0 memo carried with no workbook cell behind it; the OpEx tab now reproduces that same number as a formula chain instead of a guess. Contrast the seller's in-place bill of $25,178.65/yr (based on the pre-sale capped Taxable Value of $562,626, coincidentally already at ~SEV parity here) — the reassessment comes out to almost exactly double the seller's figure on this parcel (99.97% of 2×), but that ratio is a coincidence of this parcel's numbers, NOT a rule of thumb; the direction and magnitude depend entirely on how far the seller's pre-sale capped TV sat below the pro-forma post-sale SEV, which is why the calculation must run every time, never be assumed flat or extrapolated from one deal to the next. Add insurance, CAM/mgmt, reserves, and (if gross/MTM) utilities to reach stabilized total OpEx/SF, then test against the $4.00/SF carrying-cost floor (STAGE 1). This is the exact calculation the OpEx & Carrying Cost tab's reassessment mini-calculator performs as a formula chain: state → assessment ratio (MI = 50% SEV) → applicable cap (none survives an MI sale) → millage → reassessed tax → $/SF → carrying-cost floor test — state, price, ratio, and millage are tab INPUTS; reassessed tax, $/SF, and the CARRY-FLAGGED/OK test are tab OUTPUTS, never memo narrative.

## UNDERWRITE mode — 6-tab model integrity requirements (full text, from MODES)

UNDERWRITE: 6-tab XLSX (Inputs w/ sources, Rent Roll, 10-yr Pro Forma, Returns, Sensitivity incl. Bear/Base/Bull, **OpEx & Carrying Cost**); zero formula errors; file naming YYYYMMDD_[Address]_Model_v##.xlsx. **Stage 2.7 (Spatial/Location Analysis) fires automatically on every UNDERWRITE** — see STAGE 2.7 for scope and sourcing discipline. **Model integrity requirements (Waukee IA audit findings, resolved; OpEx tab / 3-scenario IRR / spread line added v4.0):**
  - **Formula-derived walk/floor prices:** any walk price or floor price (8%/10% YoC floor, basis floor, etc.) reported in the memo or an external planning doc MUST be goal-seeked FROM the Sensitivity tab's own formulas — never typed in as an asserted number that doesn't tie back to the workbook.
  - **Canonical stabilized NOI:** name ONE cell (e.g. Pro Forma tab `StabilizedNOI`) as the single source of truth; every other tab (Returns, Sensitivity, Summary) and the IC memo MUST reference that cell, not independently recompute NOI. A different NOI concept (e.g. Year-1 vs stabilized) must be labeled explicitly with the delta bridged — never leave two same-named NOI figures unreconciled.
  - **6th tab — OpEx & Carrying Cost (v4.0):** builds stabilized OpEx per line (property tax, insurance, CAM/mgmt, reserves, utilities-if-gross) to a stabilized total OpEx $/SF cell, and contains a reassessment mini-calculator driven by the **State Reassessment Matrix** (in `SKILL.md`): inputs = state, purchase price/basis, assessment ratio, effective millage, in-place abatement/PILOT, applicable cap (IN 3% GAV, IA 90% rollback, MD 3-yr phase-in, MI SEV≈50% uncap incl. the >50% controlling-interest trigger, MCL 211.27a(6)(h)). Output = pro-forma reassessed annual tax and $/SF, which feeds the Pro Forma tab's NOI build by formula link — never a trended seller bill pasted in. The tab tests the $4.00/SF carrying-cost floor (STAGE 1) against the computed stabilized OpEx/SF cell as a **formula** (e.g. `=IF(StabilizedOpExPerSF>4.00,"CARRY-FLAGGED","OK")`), not a narrative judgment. Every reassessment input is source-tagged/`[est]`-blue-celled per existing convention. This tab is now the single source of truth for the reassessed-tax and carrying-cost figures cited in IC Memo §6 — a hand-typed `[EST]` tax figure in the memo that doesn't tie back to this tab's cell fails the QC Gate.
  - **Unlevered 10-yr IRR floor line (v4.33):** the Returns tab computes the 10-yr unlevered IRR (all-cash, exit at the market exit cap) for Bear/Base/Bull, and tests the floor as a formula, e.g. `=IF(UnleveredIRR10<MAX(0.10,UST10+0.055),"FLOOR-FLAGGED","OK")`, where `UST10` is a source-tagged Inputs cell (FRED DGS10 30-day average + date). Levered IRR lines stay but test nothing.
  - **Rollover & credit rows (v4.33):** the Inputs tab carries the Suite-Level Rollover & Credit Block defaults (renewal probability and downtime by suite-size band, renewal TI/LC, credit-loss %, market rent growth Base/Bear/Bull) as labeled, source-tagged rows; the Rent Roll tab computes expected-value rollover per suite; the Pro Forma carries credit loss as its own line below structural vacancy; contractual escalators and market rent growth are separate Inputs cells. A model that rolls suites at 100% renewal or with no credit-loss line is **INCOMPLETE** for UNDERWRITE/FULL.
  - **Three-scenario levered IRR through debt (v4.0):** Bear/Base/Bull on the Sensitivity tab must each carry through the FULL debt-sizing (LESSER of 65% LTV and 1.25x DSCR, per UNDERWRITING CONVENTIONS) and returns waterfall to a distinct levered IRR (5-yr and 10-yr) — not just a sensitized reversion value or Year-10 NOI reported against one Base-case levered IRR. A Sensitivity tab that only re-levers Base and holds a single levered IRR across all three scenarios is **INCOMPLETE** for UNDERWRITE/FULL.
  - **Bear scenario is two distinct risk channels, not one (v4.3):** the standing Bear case has conflated a **fundamentals-bear** (vacancy up / rent down, submarket-fundamentals-driven — the input Stage 2.1 bear-mode already tracks) with a **rate/refi-shock-bear** (cap-rate expansion at exit, ALREADY partially captured by the INVERTED-SPREAD flag — AND, not yet modeled, the refi rate and resulting DSCR at the 5- or 10-year debt-maturity date). These are different risk channels with different correlation structure — a solvent, fundamentally-sound asset can still fail to refinance if rates moved against it at the rollover date, independent of vacancy/rent performance. The Sensitivity tab's Bear case must state which channel(s) it stresses; if only fundamentals are stressed, add a distinct rate/refi-shock sub-case that re-runs the refi-rate assumption (UNDERWRITING CONVENTIONS refi paragraph) at a stressed rate and reports the resulting DSCR at maturity, not just at underwriting.
  - **Vacancy as a mandatory sensitized variable:** Bear/Base/Bull on the Sensitivity tab must sensitize vacancy alongside rent and exit cap on every model — not just rent/cap — and this is load-bearing, not optional, for single-tenant vacant/lease-up deals.
  - **Insurance as a mandatory sensitized OpEx variable (v4.3):** every prior risk-modeling addition to this skill has been revenue- or cap-rate-side. Insurance has been the fastest-inflating OpEx line item across the 2020s, and ORA's footprint (OH/IN/KS/MO/TN) sits in the hail/convective-storm belt with often-older Class B/C roofs. The Bear case must sensitize the insurance line on the OpEx & Carrying Cost tab (above the standing 3% escalation default), not just rent/vacancy/exit cap — a Bear case that stresses revenue but holds OpEx flat understates tail risk on this asset class specifically.
  - **LTV-governor check:** the refi LTV assumption on the Inputs tab must be validated against the stated governor (LESSER of 65% LTV per-property and 1.25x DSCR; UNDERWRITING CONVENTIONS) via a formula/flag on the Inputs or Returns tab — not left as a free-text cell an analyst can silently override without the model surfacing a breach.
  - **Going-in / exit / spread line (v4.0):** report going-in cap / exit cap (per UNDERWRITING CONVENTIONS market table) / spread (bps) as a standing formula-derived line on the Returns tab; flag **INVERTED-SPREAD** when going-in < exit cap (see VERDICT BANDS). Reported, not scored — feeds the pre-mortem and offer strategy, never a kill.
  - **Source/rationale tags for cash-flow-affecting assumptions:** any embedded assumption that changes cash flows (e.g., an at-maturity loan rollover-on-same-terms, an above-market renewal capture rate, or a Lease-Up Assumption Block default per UNDERWRITING CONVENTIONS) must appear as a labeled row on the Inputs tab with a source/rationale note in an adjacent cell — a cell COMMENT alone is not sufficient documentation.
