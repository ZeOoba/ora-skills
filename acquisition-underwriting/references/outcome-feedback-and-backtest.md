## Annual December back-test (full text)

- **Annual December back-test** (inside the annual-cycle skill rhythm): realized YoC, basis vs RCT, leasing pace vs score components at acquisition. **Weights move ONLY at that session** (recorded exception: the v4.33 FORT Basis multiplier, ratified out of cycle by Gabriel in-turn 2026-10-18; this session re-confirms it, along with every other v4.33 constant). This is also the designated session to decide whether levered IRR (v3.8 reporting-only addition) and/or the Stage 2.3 concentration thresholds (v3.8 proposed) should convert from reported/flagged to scored/ratified. **Pre-specified methodology (v4.3) — do this every year, not an ad hoc conversation:** compare each closed deal's realized YoC/IRR and basis-vs-RCT outcome against its scorecard component scores at acquisition (Basis, Yield, Product, Market, Seller, Tenancy, VA individually, not just Combined) to see which components actually predicted realized performance — this is the evidence base any future weight change must cite, per the calibration-benchmark note below. **Also mine ORA's own ~140-tenant delinquency/renewal/collections history via `tenant-intelligence` at this session (v4.3)** for tenant-industry resilience patterns specific to small-bay multi-tenant — no verified external research on this question exists (2026-07-18 research pass came back empty), and ORA's own rent roll is the only dataset this strategy will ever have for it; feed any pattern found back into Stage 2 Tenancy scoring guidance, not as a scorecard weight change.

## Passed-deal tracking (full text)

- **Passed-deal tracking:** when a passed/flagged deal trades, log clearing price vs ORA walk to the Notion deal record ("Clearing Price" / "ORA Walk" fields) — calibrates RCT v03 and P(exec) bands. **This is now the single most valuable calibration channel we have:** RCT v03 still rests on an [EST] soft/profit/financing multiplier, and an actual clearing price is a real-world observation of what replacement-adjusted value truly is. **`ora-passed-deal-tracker` IS registered and live** (CC/MCP fleet, `30 6 15 * *` — monthly on the 15th at 06:30 ET; moved from LAMS/Cowork 2026-08-15 under workflow item T3-12; a double-fire entry guard was added 2026-08-16). *Corrected 2026-08-16 — this line previously read "No `ora-passed-deal-tracker` task is registered today", which had been false since 2026-08-15.* The weekly `ora-deal-sourcing-scan` remains a complementary channel, flagging repeat-listed / price-cut properties. Reconcile clearing prices at the annual December back-test.

**Logging a loss the moment you hear about it (T1 capture).** Gabriel learns a deal went elsewhere by **broker email or phone call** (confirmed 2026-08-16). Either path, the whole obligation is one line in any session — *"log loss: [deal], broker says it went to [X] at [$Y]"* — or forwarding the broker's email. The session then sets, on that deal's Deal Sourcing Log row: `LOI Outcome = LOST`, `Winning Price`, `Winning Price Confidence = BROKER-RELAYED` (never RECORDED-DEED from a broker's word), `Winner Entity` if named, `Loss Evidence` with the broker's name and the date, and `Status = Dead` unless a fallout position is being held. Run the R1–R6 classifier provisionally; if `ORA Max Defensible Price` is blank, the class is `UNCLASSIFIED` and stays that way.

**Saying nothing costs nothing.** T1 only accelerates and enriches — an unreported loss sits at `PENDING` and `ora-passed-deal-tracker`'s monthly Step 2a sweep catches it from the recorded deed, at higher confidence. There is no hand-kept spreadsheet anywhere in this design, and no deadline on Gabriel.

**Lost-deal post-mortem ledger (added 2026-08-16, Gabriel-ratified).** The tracker's Step 2a/4b arm additionally classifies deals ORA *bid on and lost* — a different question from an ordinary pass: a pass tests the screen, a loss tests the bid. `loi-generation` Phase 6c captures ORA's offer and its **`ORA Max Defensible Price`** (first-floor-breach price, fixed at LOI time) into the Deal Sourcing Log; the tracker later writes the winning price, winner identity/type, and a `Loss Class` under rules R1–R6. Ratified thresholds: 5% PRICE-MARGINAL band, 10% non-comparable-buyer overlay. **BASIS-DISCIPLINE is a success label** — the ledger is structurally barred from proposing a higher offer or recomputing a walk price, and weights still move only here, at the December back-test, with Gabriel's sign-off. **As of 2026-08-16 exactly ONE loss is classified**, so this channel carries a mandatory `INSUFFICIENT SAMPLE (n=X)` banner until n≥10 (≈18–36 months at ORA's LOI velocity); per-deal lessons are actionable now, distributional claims are not. **Calibration-benchmark gap (v4.3, confirmed by research):** no public index independently benchmarks ORA's actual product — NCREIF's own published industrial subtypes (Warehouse, General-Purpose Flex, R&D, Manufacturing) are each defined in ways that structurally exclude sub-50K SF, low-office, multi-tenant Class B/C shallow-bay (on top of the NPI's separate tax-exempt-institutional-owner-only composition filter). This is not a flaw in this skill's construction — no institutional benchmark for this specific strategy is known to exist anywhere. The ledger/passed-deal-tracker mechanism above is therefore not just "the most valuable" calibration channel — treat it as **the only one this strategy will ever have**, and weight the December back-test's rigor accordingly.

## Drift sweep opens the back-test (v4.14, full text — moved from `SKILL.md` OUTCOME FEEDBACK 2026-09-22, v4.23)

- **Drift sweep opens the back-test (v4.14).** The annual December back-test begins by running `market-monitor` across **all ten canonical markets** — Columbus, Cincinnati, Kansas City, Indianapolis, Madison, Maryland/DC, Grand Rapids, Richmond, Des Moines, Louisville — and collecting each market's `DOCTRINE-DRIFT` block (market-monitor v1.5) into a single divergence table. Run-triggered drift reporting only covers markets a deal happened to touch; this ensures the back-test convenes with the full picture rather than the accidental one. The sweep is a **reporting** step: constants still move only by ratification at that session. Origin: Madison carried three stale constants simultaneously — exit cap 165 bps tight, RCT v03 ~20% overstated, and a refuted owner-user pricing band — all surfaced by one deal in August 2026 and none by any scheduled process.

## Closed-deal variance review (v4.33, Gabriel 2026-10-18)

The back-test compares score components with realized YoC; it does not test the standing **defaults**. This review does.

- **When:** 12, 24 and 36 months after each close. Results are read into the December back-test; the review itself can run whenever a deal crosses one of the three marks.
- **Lines compared (underwritten vs actual):** stabilized NOI; reassessed property tax (vs the State Reassessment Matrix figure); insurance; CapEx by system (roof, paving, HVAC, doors, electrical) vs Stage 2.5 items 3–4 and the reserve line; downtime per rollover and renewal rate (vs the Suite-Level Rollover & Credit Block); TI/LC per lease; credit loss (vs 1.25%/2.0%); market rent achieved at rollover (vs the growth input).
- **Sources:** the deal's own UNDERWRITE workbook (Inputs and Pro Forma tabs, as closed — never a later re-score), PM-portal P&L and delinquency exports (`pm-portal-pnl-export`, `pm-portal-delinquency-export`), QuickBooks actuals, and the Capex & Contractor Pricing Ledger. Every actual is source-tagged; a missing actual is `[DATA-GAP]`, never estimated.
- **Output:** one variance row per line per deal (underwritten · actual · Δ · Δ% · source) appended to the back-test workbook.
- **Routing:** a standing default whose variance has the same sign and exceeds **20%** on **≥3 closed deals** `[EST]` becomes a QUESTIONS FOR GABRIEL item proposing the specific change. Never a self-edit (POST-RUN LESSON CAPTURE rule).
