## Annual December back-test (full text)

- **Annual December back-test** (inside the annual-cycle skill rhythm): realized YoC, basis vs RCT, leasing pace vs score components at acquisition. **Weights move ONLY at that session.** This is also the designated session to decide whether levered IRR (v3.8 reporting-only addition) and/or the Stage 2.3 concentration thresholds (v3.8 proposed) should convert from reported/flagged to scored/ratified. **Pre-specified methodology (v4.3) — do this every year, not an ad hoc conversation:** compare each closed deal's realized YoC/IRR and basis-vs-RCT outcome against its scorecard component scores at acquisition (Basis, Yield, Product, Market, Seller, Tenancy, VA individually, not just Combined) to see which components actually predicted realized performance — this is the evidence base any future weight change must cite, per the calibration-benchmark note below. **Also mine ORA's own ~140-tenant delinquency/renewal/collections history via `tenant-intelligence` at this session (v4.3)** for tenant-industry resilience patterns specific to small-bay multi-tenant — no verified external research on this question exists (2026-07-18 research pass came back empty), and ORA's own rent roll is the only dataset this strategy will ever have for it; feed any pattern found back into Stage 2 Tenancy scoring guidance, not as a scorecard weight change.

## Passed-deal tracking (full text)

- **Passed-deal tracking:** when a passed/flagged deal trades, log clearing price vs ORA walk to the Notion deal record ("Clearing Price" / "ORA Walk" fields) — calibrates RCT v03 and P(exec) bands. **This is now the single most valuable calibration channel we have:** RCT v03 still rests on an [EST] soft/profit/financing multiplier, and an actual clearing price is a real-world observation of what replacement-adjusted value truly is. **`ora-passed-deal-tracker` IS registered and live** (CC/MCP fleet, `30 6 15 * *` — monthly on the 15th at 06:30 ET; moved from LAMS/Cowork 2026-08-15 under workflow item T3-12; a double-fire entry guard was added 2026-08-16). *Corrected 2026-08-16 — this line previously read "No `ora-passed-deal-tracker` task is registered today", which had been false since 2026-08-15.* The weekly `ora-deal-sourcing-scan` remains a complementary channel, flagging repeat-listed / price-cut properties. Reconcile clearing prices at the annual December back-test.

**Lost-deal post-mortem ledger (added 2026-08-16, Gabriel-ratified).** The tracker's Step 2a/4b arm additionally classifies deals ORA *bid on and lost* — a different question from an ordinary pass: a pass tests the screen, a loss tests the bid. `loi-generation` Phase 6c captures ORA's offer and its **`ORA Max Defensible Price`** (first-floor-breach price, fixed at LOI time) into the Deal Sourcing Log; the tracker later writes the winning price, winner identity/type, and a `Loss Class` under rules R1–R6. Ratified thresholds: 5% PRICE-MARGINAL band, 10% non-comparable-buyer overlay. **BASIS-DISCIPLINE is a success label** — the ledger is structurally barred from proposing a higher offer or recomputing a walk price, and weights still move only here, at the December back-test, with Gabriel's sign-off. **As of 2026-08-16 exactly ONE loss is classified**, so this channel carries a mandatory `INSUFFICIENT SAMPLE (n=X)` banner until n≥10 (≈18–36 months at ORA's LOI velocity); per-deal lessons are actionable now, distributional claims are not. **Calibration-benchmark gap (v4.3, confirmed by research):** no public index independently benchmarks ORA's actual product — NCREIF's own published industrial subtypes (Warehouse, General-Purpose Flex, R&D, Manufacturing) are each defined in ways that structurally exclude sub-50K SF, low-office, multi-tenant Class B/C shallow-bay (on top of the NPI's separate tax-exempt-institutional-owner-only composition filter). This is not a flaw in this skill's construction — no institutional benchmark for this specific strategy is known to exist anywhere. The ledger/passed-deal-tracker mechanism above is therefore not just "the most valuable" calibration channel — treat it as **the only one this strategy will ever have**, and weight the December back-test's rigor accordingly.
