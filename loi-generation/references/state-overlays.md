# State Overlays for §6 Pro-rations, Costs, and Transfer Taxes

Each overlay replaces the §6 paragraphs in the master template body. The first sentence (operating expenses prorated at 11:59 p.m. day prior to Closing) and the final sentence (each party pays own attorneys' fees) remain in all overlays. The middle proration-method and cost-allocation language changes by state.

---

## OH · Ohio (DEFAULT in master template)

Operating expenses, service contracts, utilities, and similar items prorate as of 11:59 p.m. on the day before Closing. Real estate taxes prorate using the "long proration" method customary in {{PROPERTY_COUNTY}}, Ohio: Seller credits Buyer at Closing for (i) any unpaid taxes for tax years preceding the year of Closing, and (ii) the portion of the current calendar year's taxes accrued through (but not including) the Closing Date, calculated from the most recent ascertainable tax bill. Buyer retains the right, at Buyer's expense, to file Board of Revision complaints for the year of Closing and forward, and to retain any refund attributable to post-Closing periods. Special assessments certified before Closing are Seller's; those certified after Closing are Buyer's.

Seller pays: all Ohio state and {{PROPERTY_COUNTY}} real estate conveyance fees imposed by ORC §§ 319.54(G)(3) and 322.02; the owner's title insurance policy (standard coverage); one-half of escrow and closing fees; the cost of releasing any monetary liens; and recording costs for any release of Seller's mortgage(s). Buyer pays: title policy endorsements; deed recording; one-half of escrow and closing fees; and any new ALTA survey. Each party pays its own attorneys' fees and wire transfer fees.

## MD · Maryland

Operating expenses, service contracts, utilities, and similar items prorate as of 11:59 p.m. on the day before Closing. Real estate taxes prorate on a calendar-year basis through the Closing Date based on the most recent ascertainable tax bill. Special assessments certified before Closing are Seller's; those certified after Closing are Buyer's.

Seller and Buyer will each pay one-half of the Maryland state recordation tax and the {{PROPERTY_COUNTY}} county transfer tax, in accordance with Maryland Real Property Article §13-203 customary practice; provided that if {{PROPERTY_COUNTY}} customarily allocates these costs differently (e.g., Montgomery County allowing Buyer-bears or Seller-bears variants), the customary local allocation will govern. Seller pays the owner's title insurance policy (standard coverage); one-half of escrow and closing fees; the cost of releasing any monetary liens; and recording costs for any release of Seller's mortgage(s). Buyer pays title policy endorsements; deed recording; one-half of escrow and closing fees; and any new ALTA survey. Each party pays its own attorneys' fees and wire transfer fees.

## IN · Indiana

Operating expenses, service contracts, utilities, and similar items prorate as of 11:59 p.m. on the day before Closing. Real estate taxes in Indiana are billed and paid in arrears; taxes prorate at Closing with Seller crediting Buyer for (i) any unpaid taxes for tax years preceding the year of Closing, and (ii) the portion of the current calendar year's taxes accrued through (but not including) the Closing Date, calculated from the most recent ascertainable tax bill. Special assessments certified before Closing are Seller's; those certified after Closing are Buyer's. Indiana has no state real estate transfer tax.

Seller and Buyer will jointly execute the Indiana Sales Disclosure Form required under IC § 6-1.1-5.5 at Closing.
