# Guaranty Exhibit — Long Form

Use only when the guarantor's credit is thin (financial-statement covenant needed to monitor
it) or a confessed-judgment provision is contemplated. Adds to the short form (all six lettered
paragraphs A–F carry over unchanged) rather than replacing it:

- **G. Financial-Statement Covenant.** Guarantor shall deliver to Landlord, within 90 days after
  each fiscal year end, a current personal financial statement in a form reasonably acceptable
  to Landlord, certified by Guarantor as true and complete, and shall deliver such additional
  financial information as Landlord reasonably requests in connection with a proposed
  assignment, financing, or sale of the Project.
- **H. Notices.** Notices to Guarantor shall be given in the manner and to the address specified
  for notices to Tenant under the Lease, or to [GUARANTOR NOTICE ADDRESS] if different, and are
  effective as provided in the Lease's notice provision.
- **I. Representations.** Guarantor represents that Guarantor has the full legal capacity and
  authority to execute this Guaranty, that this Guaranty is a valid and binding obligation of
  Guarantor, and that Guarantor has received a copy of the Lease and had the opportunity to
  review it with counsel of Guarantor's choosing.
- **J. Notarial Acknowledgment.** This Guaranty is acknowledged before a notary public in the
  form required by [PROPERTY STATE] law for an instrument of this kind — see the state overlay
  for the applicable acknowledgment certificate.

**Confessed judgment.** [PROPERTY STATE]-specific — verify before drafting; do not draft a
confession-of-judgment clause from this template without counsel review. Maryland: see
Md. Rule 2-611 in `references/state-overlay.json` for the confessed-judgment procedure this
would invoke; the rule governs enforcement procedure, not the drafting of the underlying clause
— counsel drafts the clause itself. If the state overlay marks this
`UNVERIFIED — counsel confirmation required`, the QC gate surfaces it and the draft may not
present as ready with a confessed-judgment clause in place until counsel clears it.
